## Goal (owner)
Local Cooks is a commercial-kitchen rental marketplace. Kitchens must trust it enough to pay a subscription, so every
screen is judged against The Food Corridor (weighted as much as Airbnb), Airbnb, Peerspace and Turo. The kitchen side got
the full pass through Oct 24. **Next: the chef (customer) side, at the same level.**

## How the owner wants the work done
- **Dev only.** Nothing goes to production until the owner's team has tested dev and a lawyer has reviewed the terms.
- **Research first, then recommend.** Look at what the top companies actually do (help centres, screens), say what you
  found and recommend one option. Ask when a decision belongs to the owner; never assume. Never change who/when/how much
  (rules, money, policy) on your own.
- **Skills:**
  - UI: taste-skill (design-taste-frontend) for a new base, then impeccable to refine, one after the other. Small fixes:
    impeccable only. Show the owner a mock before replacing a whole screen.
  - Customer-facing words (UI copy, emails, terms): no-ai-slop. Self-check every draft against it.
  - Payments, refunds, payouts, holds, auth: vibe-security.
  - Chat with the owner: caveman voice. Code: ponytail (smallest complete change).
- **Look at every screen you change,** desktop (1440×900) and phone (iPhone 13), including menus, dialogs, empty, error
  and success states. Tests passing is not the same as the screen being clear; report the two separately.
- **Tests and tsc in the background** when you are only waiting on them. tsc takes 10–20 min; run one at a time.
- **Batch deploys:** verify locally, push a meaningful batch to `development`, then watch the Vercel status
  (`gh api repos/:owner/:repo/commits/<sha>/status`) and check the change live on dev.
- **Log as you go:** add a dated section to `PHASE0_LOG.md` for each batch (owner decisions, built, verified, open).
- The owner often adds messages mid-task saying "not trying to interrupt". Finish the current item first, then pick up
  the new one; keep a list so none get lost.

## Wording rules (owner)
- Vocabulary: "Access request" (whole thing), "Request to apply" (step 1), "Booking requirements" (step 2 and the
  kitchen's settings page).
- Never tell customers or kitchens that Local Cooks approves the first request; it reads as going to the kitchen.
- Kitchen-side copy says the customer's name, else "customer" (never "chef"). The chef side keeps "chef" where natural.
- No raw emails on booking pages; contact is in-app messaging. One phone per account, shown only once a booking is
  confirmed.
- No one-tap consequential actions: row actions open the review screen.
- No "goodwill" wording for customers.

## Chef side: where to start
Nothing has been decided yet. Ask the owner which part comes first, then audit it against peers before you change it.
Areas, in the order a chef meets them:
1. Sign-up and sign-in (6-digit code, password optional), and the v3.0 terms agree screen for chefs.
2. Discovering kitchens: the kitchen page, photos, price and rules shown before booking.
3. "Request to cook": one page for signed-in and signed-out users (built Oct 23), plus Booking requirements (step 2) and
   renewals.
4. The booking wizard and Stripe hold, the "request sent" page, My Bookings, and the booking page before, during and
   after a visit (check-in code, extra time, checkout, claims, receipts).
