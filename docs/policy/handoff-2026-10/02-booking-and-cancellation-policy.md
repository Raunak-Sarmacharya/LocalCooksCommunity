# Kitchen Booking & Cancellation Policy

**Where:** new page `/policies/bookings`. Link it from Terms §4.7 and §5.1, from the "Cancellation" line at checkout, and from every
booking page. All amounts are in Canadian dollars. Times are shown in the kitchen's time zone.

---

## 1. Booking a kitchen

You can book kitchens that have approved your application to use them. Each kitchen sets its own hourly (or daily) rate, the
shortest and longest booking it accepts, how far ahead you must book, and how many days one booking can cover. Before you pay, you
see the full price: kitchen time, any equipment you add, HST at the kitchen's rate, and the Local Cooks service fee.

**Other limits a kitchen may set.**
- **A buffer between bookings** — none, 1 hour or 2 hours. The kitchen keeps this time free between two different bookings. You
  can't choose hours inside it. It doesn't change bookings already made, and the kitchen can still book inside it itself.
- **A weekly limit for each chef** — for example 8 hours a week. It counts the whole week, Monday to Sunday in the kitchen's time
  zone, including hours you've already booked at that kitchen. A full day counts as that day's opening hours, and hours you
  cancelled don't count. If you reach the limit, the booking page tells you; the kitchen can override it.
- **A longest booking** — for example 4 hours per day. This applies to hourly bookings. A full day at the kitchen's daily rate is
  always bookable.

**Requests and card holds.** Booking sends a request to the kitchen and places a **hold** on your card for the total. You are
charged only if the kitchen accepts — and if it can't provide an item, only after you approve the new total. If the kitchen
declines, or doesn't answer in time, the hold is released and you pay nothing. A released hold is not a refund — no money was taken
— but your bank decides how fast the hold disappears from your available balance.

**The kitchen's answer.** The kitchen answers within 24 hours, and always before its required notice ahead of your start. If you
still have free cancellation, the kitchen must answer at least 1 hour before your free cancellation ends. If you book so close to
the start that this isn't possible, we tell you before you pay.

**If the kitchen can't provide something.** The kitchen can accept your request without an item, for example a piece of equipment.
It can't change your booking on its own: it asks you to approve a new total, and nothing is charged until you accept.

We tell you in the app and by email: "{kitchen} can host you, but can't provide {items}. New total ${new} (was ${old}). Accept or
decline by {time}." Your days stay reserved while you decide, and the kitchen sees "Waiting for the chef".

You have 24 hours to answer, or until 1 hour before your refund deadline if that comes sooner. If you accept, we charge only the
new total and release the rest of the hold. If you decline, or don't answer, the request closes and the whole hold is released. If
your card hold ends first, the request closes the same way and nothing is charged.

**When your booking starts soon.** If there isn't time for you to answer — less than 3 hours, or an answer deadline that would
fall after your first booked start — the kitchen can't ask you to approve a new total. It can accept the full request, or decline
it and message you.

**Invitations.** A kitchen can invite you to a specific time at a price it sets. You can add equipment, then pay; the booking is
confirmed as soon as you pay. An invitation you don't pay by its expiry closes, and nothing is charged.

**Withdrawing a request.** Until the kitchen accepts, you can withdraw your request at any time. The whole hold is released.

## 2. Booking several days

One booking can cover more than one day. Pick any days you like — they don't have to be next to each other — up to the kitchen's
limit (no more than 14 days), all within 14 days of your first day. You send one request, and we hold your card for the whole
booking. The kitchen accepts all the days or declines the whole request; if it declines, it can message you or invite you to other
days. When it accepts everything, we charge the full total. If it can host you but can't provide an item, we ask you to approve the
new total first (see above).

**Each day is its own visit.** For each day, choose a full day at the daily rate or your own hours at the hourly rate. The same
choice applies to every day by default, and you can change any day. The kitchen's shortest and longest booking limits apply to each
day. A day the kitchen is closed, already booked, under maintenance, too soon, or beyond how far ahead it takes bookings can't be
picked, and the page tells you why. Each day has its own check-in and check-out, and its own check-in code.

**Equipment.** Equipment is added for every day by default, and you can untick an item on any day. You pay the item's price for
each day it's booked. A day only offers equipment that is free that day.

**One refund deadline for the whole booking.** It's the kitchen's notice period before your **first** day. Dropping your first day
doesn't move it.
- **Before it,** you can drop any day (your booking can have gaps) or cancel the whole booking. You're refunded for that part — its
  kitchen time, equipment, HST and service fee — minus the card processing fee on that part.
- **After it,** a day you drop follows the kitchen's late charge: at a 50% kitchen you get back half of that day's paid share,
  minus the card processing fee on that half; at a 100% kitchen you get nothing back for that day. A kitchen can always choose to
  refund more.
- **A day that has started, or that you missed, isn't refundable.** Cancelling the whole booking after the deadline means the
  remaining days.

**Changing the whole booking.** One change request covers the days and each day's hours, still within 14 days of the first day.
You see the price difference before you send it, and your booking stays as it is until the kitchen answers. You can ask before your
refund deadline.

**If the kitchen cancels one day.** You get that day back in full, including its card processing fee, and the kitchen isn't paid for
it; Local Cooks covers that fee. You can then drop the remaining days without the late charge, until the next booked day starts.
Each of those days is refunded minus its card processing fee, and the refund comes out of the kitchen's payout for that day.

**Invitations.** A kitchen can invite you to several days at once, on the same rules, at a reduced price it offers you.

## 3. Changing a booking

**Before the kitchen answers:** send a changed request (new days, time, length or equipment). Your earlier request is withdrawn
and its hold released only once the new one is sent, so you never lose your place by trying to change it.

**After the kitchen confirms:** until your refund deadline, you can ask to move the booking to another date or time, change its
length, or for a multi-day booking change the days or any day's hours. One change request covers the whole booking. You see the
price difference and your new refund deadline before you send the request. The kitchen approves or declines it; until then your
booking stays as it is.
- If the new booking costs more, the difference is held on your card and charged only if the kitchen approves.
- If it costs less, the difference is refunded when the kitchen approves, minus the card processing fee on that difference.
- Your refund deadline moves with the new time (for a multi-day booking, with the new first day).

**Removing equipment from a confirmed booking:** before your refund deadline, you are refunded for that item at once (its price,
HST and service fee, minus the card processing fee on that part). After the deadline, the item follows your booking's
late-cancellation charge. Equipment that came free with the kitchen is removed with no money changing hands. On a multi-day
booking, you can remove an item from any day that hasn't started, under the same rules for that day.

## 4. Cancelling

**Every kitchen chooses one cancellation policy:** a notice period of 24, 48 or 72 hours before the start, and a late charge of 50%
or 100%. You see it before you book. **The policy in force when you book is saved with your booking** — if the kitchen changes its
policy later, your booking keeps the one you agreed to.

| When you cancel a confirmed booking | What you get back |
|---|---|
| Before the notice period starts (your refund deadline) | Everything you paid — kitchen time, equipment, HST and the Local Cooks service fee — **minus the actual card processing fee** for your payment. We show that fee in dollars before you confirm. |
| After your refund deadline, at a 50% kitchen | Half of the booking total (minus the card processing fee on that half) |
| After your refund deadline, at a 100% kitchen | No refund |
| You don't show up | No refund, at any kitchen |
| **The kitchen cancels** | A full refund, **including** the card processing fee |

For a multi-day booking, there is one refund deadline — the notice period before your first day — and dropped days follow the rules
in "Booking several days".

The card processing fee is the real cost of processing your card payment; we don't add a cancellation fee on top. If you book after
your refund deadline has already passed, we tell you before you pay that you'll owe the late charge once the kitchen confirms.

**Refund timing.** Refunds are sent immediately. Banks usually show them within 5–10 business days.

## 5. Your visit

**Arrival details** (address details, entry instructions, contact) are shared 48 hours before your booking. For a multi-day
booking, they're shared 48 hours before your first day, and each day has its own check-in.

**Check-in** opens 15 minutes before your start (the kitchen can set a different window). At staffed kitchens, ask the staff for
your 6-digit check-in code and enter it in Local Cooks; some kitchens let you check yourself in. Arriving late doesn't move your end
time.

**Starting early** is possible only if the kitchen offers it.

**Extra time.** While you're checked in you can ask for more time. The kitchen can approve it with a charge, approve it for free, or
decline it. If there's a charge, your card is held and charged only when the kitchen approves. Only time that is actually free is
offered. Extra time can't be refunded once it's added. A request the kitchen doesn't answer within 15 minutes expires. On a
multi-day booking, extra time works the same way for the day you're checked in, priced at that day's rate.

**Staying past your time.** If you stay longer without extra time, the kitchen can record when you left and send you a charge for
the extra time in 30-minute blocks. **You pay it or dispute it — it is never charged automatically.** Charges not resolved within
48 hours go to Local Cooks.

**Leaving early** doesn't refund the time you booked but didn't use.

**If you can't get in, or something is unsafe,** use "Report a problem" from the check-in screen. The kitchen and Local Cooks are
told at once, and you can message the kitchen right there. **In an emergency, call 911 first.**

## 6. After your visit

The kitchen inspects the space within 24 hours of your departure. On a multi-day booking, each day is inspected and closes on its
own. If the kitchen finds damage or extra cleaning, it can report it within 72 hours. Local Cooks reviews every report before
you're contacted — see the Damage & Cleaning Claims policy.

If a kitchen reports that you didn't attend, you can answer "I was there" and Local Cooks will review it. The kitchen's report
doesn't move any money by itself.

## 7. What kitchens agree to

- Keep the listing, prices, hours, equipment and rules accurate, and answer requests on time.
- Give access at the booked time, as described in the listing and arrival details.
- Use the cancellation policy shown to the chef; never ask a chef to pay outside Local Cooks.
- If the kitchen cancels a confirmed booking, the chef is refunded in full.
- Accept all the days of a multi-day request or decline it. If the kitchen cancels one day, that day is refunded in full including
  the card processing fee, and the chef can drop the remaining days without the late charge.
- If the kitchen can't provide an item, ask the chef to approve the new total — never change the booking without their answer.
