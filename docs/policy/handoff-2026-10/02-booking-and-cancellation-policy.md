# Kitchen Booking & Cancellation Policy

**Where:** new page `/policies/bookings`. Link it from Terms §4.7 and §5.1, from the "Cancellation" line at checkout, and from every
booking page. All amounts are in Canadian dollars. Times are shown in the kitchen's time zone.

---

## 1. Booking a kitchen

You can book kitchens that have approved your application to use them. Each kitchen sets its own hourly (or daily) rate, the
shortest and longest booking it accepts, and how far ahead you must book. Before you pay, you see the full price: kitchen time,
any equipment you add, HST at the kitchen's rate, and the Local Cooks service fee.

**Requests and card holds.** Booking sends a request to the kitchen and places a **hold** on your card for the total. You are
charged only if the kitchen accepts. If the kitchen declines, or doesn't answer in time, the hold is released and you pay nothing.
A released hold is not a refund — no money was taken — but your bank decides how fast the hold disappears from your available
balance.

**The kitchen's answer.** The kitchen answers within 24 hours, and always before its required notice ahead of your start. If you
still have free cancellation, the kitchen must answer at least 1 hour before your free cancellation ends. If you book so close to
the start that this isn't possible, we tell you before you pay. A kitchen may accept your kitchen time and decline an item (for
example a piece of equipment); you are charged only for what it accepts.

**Invitations.** A kitchen can invite you to a specific time at a price it sets. You can add equipment, then pay; the booking is
confirmed as soon as you pay. An invitation you don't pay by its expiry closes, and nothing is charged.

**Withdrawing a request.** Until the kitchen accepts, you can withdraw your request at any time. The whole hold is released.

## 2. Changing a booking

**Before the kitchen answers:** send a changed request (new time, length or equipment). Your earlier request is withdrawn and its
hold released only once the new one is sent, so you never lose your place by trying to change it.

**After the kitchen confirms:** until your refund deadline, you can ask to move the booking to another date or time, or change its
length. You see the price difference and your new refund deadline before you send the request. The kitchen approves or declines it;
until then your booking stays as it is.
- If the new booking costs more, the difference is held on your card and charged only if the kitchen approves.
- If it costs less, the difference is refunded when the kitchen approves, minus the card processing fee on that difference.
- Your refund deadline moves with the new time.

**Removing equipment from a confirmed booking:** before your refund deadline, you are refunded for that item at once (its price,
HST and service fee, minus the card processing fee on that part). After the deadline, the item follows your booking's late-cancellation
charge. Equipment that came free with the kitchen is removed with no money changing hands.

## 3. Cancelling

**Every kitchen chooses one cancellation policy:** a notice period of 24, 48 or 72 hours before the start, and a late charge of 50%
or 100%. You see it before you book. **The policy in force when you book is saved with your booking** — if the kitchen changes its
policy later, your booking keeps the one you agreed to.

| When you cancel a confirmed booking | What you get back |
|---|---|
| Before the notice period starts (your refund deadline) | Everything you paid — kitchen time, equipment, HST and the Local Cooks service fee — **minus the actual card processing fee** for your payment. We show that fee in dollars before you confirm. |
| After your refund deadline, at a 50% kitchen | Half of the booking total (minus the card processing fee on that half) |
| After your refund deadline, at a 100% kitchen | No refund |
| You don't show up | No refund, at any kitchen |
| **The kitchen cancels** | A full refund, **including** the card processing fee |

The card processing fee is the real cost of processing your card payment; we don't add a cancellation fee on top. If you book after
your refund deadline has already passed, we tell you before you pay that you'll owe the late charge once the kitchen confirms.

**Refund timing.** Refunds are sent immediately. Banks usually show them within 5–10 business days.

## 4. Your visit

**Arrival details** (address details, entry instructions, contact) are shared 48 hours before your booking.

**Check-in** opens 15 minutes before your start (the kitchen can set a different window). At staffed kitchens, ask the staff for
your 6-digit check-in code and enter it in Local Cooks; some kitchens let you check yourself in. Arriving late doesn't move your end
time.

**Starting early** is possible only if the kitchen offers it.

**Extra time.** While you're checked in you can ask for more time. The kitchen can approve it with a charge, approve it for free, or
decline it. If there's a charge, your card is held and charged only when the kitchen approves. Only time that is actually free is
offered. Extra time can't be refunded once it's added. A request the kitchen doesn't answer within 15 minutes expires.

**Staying past your time.** If you stay longer without extra time, the kitchen can record when you left and send you a charge for
the extra time in 30-minute blocks. **You pay it or dispute it — it is never charged automatically.** Charges not resolved within
48 hours go to Local Cooks.

**Leaving early** doesn't refund the time you booked but didn't use.

**If you can't get in, or something is unsafe,** use "Report a problem" from the check-in screen. The kitchen and Local Cooks are
told at once, and you can message the kitchen right there. **In an emergency, call 911 first.**

## 5. After your visit

The kitchen inspects the space within 24 hours of your departure. If it finds damage or extra cleaning, it can report it within 72
hours. Local Cooks reviews every report before you're contacted — see the Damage & Cleaning Claims policy.

If a kitchen reports that you didn't attend, you can answer "I was there" and Local Cooks will review it. The kitchen's report
doesn't move any money by itself.

## 6. What kitchens agree to

- Keep the listing, prices, hours, equipment and rules accurate, and answer requests on time.
- Give access at the booked time, as described in the listing and arrival details.
- Use the cancellation policy shown to the chef; never ask a chef to pay outside Local Cooks.
- If the kitchen cancels a confirmed booking, the chef is refunded in full.
