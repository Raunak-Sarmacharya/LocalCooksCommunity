# Payments, Fees & Payouts

**Where:** new page `/policies/payments`. Link it from Terms §4.9 and §9. All amounts are in Canadian dollars. Payments are processed
by Stripe; kitchens are paid through Stripe Connect.

---

## For chefs

**What you pay.** Before every payment you see the full amount: the kitchen's price (kitchen time, equipment or storage days), HST at
the kitchen's rate, and the Local Cooks service fee. Local Cooks may charge a service fee; it is always shown before you pay and is
never added afterwards.

**Holds, not charges.** For requests (bookings, storage rentals, booking changes that cost more, paid extra time), we place a hold on
your card. You're charged only when the kitchen approves. Declined, expired and withdrawn requests release the hold.

**Your card on file.** Stripe keeps the card you pay with so you can use it again **for things you confirm yourself**:
- extending a storage rental (one tap);
- extra time you asked for, when the kitchen approves it with a charge;
- a holdover charge or a damage or cleaning claim you agree to pay.

We never charge a saved card on our own: no automatic renewals, no automatic penalties, no automatic claims. You can always choose
another card.

**Refunds.** Refunds go back to the card you paid with, are sent immediately, and usually show within 5–10 business days. When a
refund follows your own cancellation or change, the actual card processing fee for that payment isn't refunded (we show it in
dollars first). When the kitchen cancels, you get everything back, including that fee. See the Booking & Cancellation and Storage
Rental policies for when refunds apply.

**Unpaid claims.** If Local Cooks upholds a damage or cleaning claim and it is still unpaid 7 days after the decision, you can't make
new bookings until it's paid or resolved. Bookings you already have aren't affected.

**Card disputes (chargebacks).** If something went wrong, contact us first — most problems are solved faster that way. If you dispute
a payment with your bank, refunds on that booking or rental are paused while the bank decides, and Local Cooks answers the dispute
with the booking's records. Disputing payments for services you received may lead to your account being suspended.

## For kitchens

**What you receive.** For each booking, storage term or extra-time charge, you receive what the chef paid **minus the Local Cooks
service fee and the actual card processing fee**. HST collected from the chef is included in your payout; you are responsible for
remitting it. Your rental page always shows "You'll be paid $X on {date}".

**When you're paid.**

| What | When the payout is sent |
|---|---|
| Kitchen booking (and extra time added to it) | As soon as you finish the inspection, or automatically 24 hours after the visit ends |
| Storage | At the end of each paid term (each extension pays out at its own end) — or as soon as you clear the chef's move-out. If the move-out clears automatically, the payout waits until the 72-hour claim window has passed. |
| Storage holdover the chef paid | At once |
| Approved damage or cleaning claim | The full approved amount; Local Cooks covers the card processing fee. A claim never holds the booking's own payout. |

Payouts go to your Stripe account; Stripe then pays your bank on your Stripe payout schedule (usually within a few business days).

**When a payout is held.** A payout waits while a problem report, damage claim or Local Cooks review is open on that booking or rental,
and is released as soon as it's resolved.

**Refunds you fund.** If you cancel a booking, ask a storage renter to move out early, or give a refund yourself, that refund comes out
of your payout. If you were already paid, the amount is recorded as owed to Local Cooks and we'll contact you to arrange it.

**Chargebacks.** Local Cooks answers chargebacks with the booking's records. [Owner to confirm: "During our pilot, a chargeback doesn't
reduce your payout for a session or rental that was delivered."]

**Stripe account.** To be paid, you connect a Stripe account and accept Stripe's Connected Account Agreement. Stripe verifies your
identity and bank details.

## Taxes

Prices are shown with HST at the rate the kitchen sets. Local Cooks collects that HST as part of the chef's payment and passes it to
the kitchen; the kitchen remits it. Receipts and payout statements show the HST separately. [Accountant to confirm the treatment of
the Local Cooks service fee for HST.]
