# Damage & Cleaning Claims, Problems and Disputes

**Where:** replace the text of the claims component shown inside the Terms (`client/src/components/legal/DamageCleaningPolicy.tsx`,
keys `dcV2Policy*` / `dcV2ManagerPolicy*` in `booking.json`) with this, and publish it at `/policies/claims`. The kitchen-booking rules
below are what the product already does; the storage and problem sections are new.

---

## 1. Damage and extra cleaning

**Who can claim, and when.** A kitchen can report damage or extra cleaning:
- after a **kitchen booking**: within 72 hours of the chef's departure (or the booked end, if no departure was recorded);
- after a **storage move-out**: within 72 hours of the chef's move-out request.
One claim per visit or move-out.

**What can be claimed.** Repair, replacement or extra cleaning, up to **$250** in the app, with a receipt, invoice or quote for each
cost. Fines and lost revenue can't be claimed. For larger amounts, contact Local Cooks.

**Evidence.** Photos and notes from arrival and departure (or move-in, handover and move-out) are attached automatically. If no
move-in photos were taken, the claim says so.

**Local Cooks reviews every claim first.** We update the kitchen within 2 business days. We may ask the kitchen for more
information, reduce the amount, close the claim, or pay the kitchen ourselves at no cost to the chef. Only approved claims are sent
to the chef. **What the kitchen tells Local Cooks during the review isn't shown to the chef.**

**The chef's choice.** The chef has 72 hours to pay (with a saved or new card, confirmed by the chef) or dispute with a note and
photos. Disputed and unanswered claims return to Local Cooks for a final decision. **A card is never charged automatically for a
claim.**

**If an upheld claim stays unpaid** 7 days after the final decision, the chef can't make new bookings until it's paid or resolved.
Existing bookings and rentals aren't affected. The pause ends when the claim is paid, rejected or written off, or when Local Cooks
releases it.

**Money.** The kitchen receives the full approved amount; Local Cooks covers the card processing fee. A claim doesn't hold the
booking's or rental's own payout. Local Cooks can withdraw a claim at no cost to either person.

## 2. Storage holdover charges

If a chef keeps storage more than 5 days past their paid-through date, the kitchen may ask them to pay for the extra days at the daily
price. The chef pays or disputes within 72 hours; Local Cooks decides disputes. Nothing is charged automatically. (See the Storage
Rental Policy.)

## 3. Problems during a booking or rental

Either side can **report a problem** — for example the chef can't get in, equipment is broken, a storage unit is at the wrong
temperature, or something is unsafe.
- The kitchen, the chef and Local Cooks see the report and can reply in it.
- The kitchen's payout for that booking or rental is held until Local Cooks resolves the report.
- **In an emergency, call 911 first.**

## 4. No-shows

If a kitchen reports that a chef didn't attend, nothing is refunded (no-shows aren't refunded at any kitchen). The chef can answer
"I was there", which goes to Local Cooks as a problem report. The kitchen's report doesn't move any money by itself.

## 5. How Local Cooks decides

We decide on the records in Local Cooks: the booking or rental, check-in and check-out times, photos and notes, messages, receipts and
both sides' statements. We may contact either side for more information. Our decision on a claim, holdover dispute or problem report
is final within Local Cooks; it doesn't limit any legal rights you have.
