# Storage Rental Policy

**Where:** new page `/policies/storage`. Link it from Terms §5.4 and §9.4, from the "How storage works" box on the storage request
page, and from every storage rental page. All amounts are in Canadian dollars; dates follow the kitchen's time zone.

---

## 1. How storage works

Kitchens rent out dry, cooler and freezer storage **by the day**, separately from kitchen time. A listing has a number of units
(for example "Dry shelf · 4 units"). When you rent, you get one unit for the days you choose. Several chefs can rent the same
listing until every unit is taken; you'll see "Available from {date}" when it's full.

You can rent storage at kitchens that have approved your application to use them.

**No automatic charges.** Storage never renews on its own. You choose the days and pay for them up front. To keep your space longer,
you extend it yourself.

## 2. Renting

- Choose a start date within the next 14 days and how many days you need (1 to 30, or fewer if the kitchen sets a lower limit).
- You see the daily price, the number of days, HST, the Local Cooks service fee and the total before you pay.
- Your card is **held** for the total, and the kitchen answers within 24 hours. You're charged only if the kitchen approves. If it
  declines or doesn't answer in time, the hold is released.
- You can withdraw a request at no cost until the kitchen answers.

## 3. Extending

When 14 days or fewer of your paid time remain, you can extend by tapping **Extend**, choosing the days, and paying the total shown.
Stripe keeps the card you rented with on file so you can pay in one tap; it's charged **only when you tap to pay**. If your bank
asks for a security check, you complete it on the spot. If the card is declined, you can pay with another card. You can't extend past
another chef's reservation of the same unit, or past a date the kitchen has asked you to move out by.

## 4. Moving in and out

- **Move in** on or after your start date. Add photos of the empty space if you can — they protect you if a damage question comes up
  later. If you don't, any later claim will note "No move-in photos were taken."
- **Move out** whenever you're done: remove everything and add photos. The kitchen then checks the space. If it doesn't, the move-out
  clears on its own after the review window.
- **Ending early isn't refunded.** The kitchen may choose to refund unused days.

## 5. Cancelling before your start date

Once the kitchen has approved your rental, cancelling before the start follows **the same cancellation policy the kitchen uses for
kitchen bookings** (its notice period of 24, 48 or 72 hours and its 50% or 100% late charge), counted back from your start date. A
refund is everything you paid minus the actual card processing fee. If you never move in, the kitchen may release the unit from 7
days after your start date; that isn't refunded. If you were there, use "Report a problem" and Local Cooks will review it.

## 6. After your paid time ends

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Up to 5 days after your paid-through date | **Grace period:** you can still extend at the same daily price. |
| From day 6 | **Holdover:** the kitchen may ask you to pay for the extra days at the same daily price (plus HST), or waive them. **You pay it yourself or dispute it within 72 hours — it is never charged automatically.** Disputes are decided by Local Cooks. |
| From 15 days of holdover | Local Cooks opens a **removal review** and contacts you. Holdover stops adding up. Moving out or extending (paying for every day through today) ends the review. |

## 7. If the kitchen needs the space back

A kitchen can ask you to move out by a date:
- **Rentals of 7 days or fewer** can't be shortened by the kitchen (Local Cooks can step in for emergencies).
- **Longer rentals** get at least 7 days' notice (or the time left, if less).
- **Every unused paid day is refunded to you in full**, including its share of the service fee. The kitchen pays for that refund.

A kitchen can also move you to another unit of the same listing (your dates and price stay the same), or offer you more days.

## 8. Items left behind

If a rental reaches removal review, Local Cooks sends you a **final notice** listing the items in the space, with photos.
- Perishable items may be removed **48 hours** after the final notice.
- Other items may be removed **7 days** after the final notice.
- Items are never sold. The kitchen records what it removed, with photos.

> Owner/lawyer: removal by the kitchen is switched **off** in the product until you approve this section. Until then, only Local Cooks
> records removals.

## 9. Problems, damage and cleaning

- **Report a problem** on your rental page at any time (for example you can't get in, or the temperature is wrong). The kitchen and
  Local Cooks see it, and the kitchen's payout for your rental is held until it's resolved.
- After you move out, the kitchen has **72 hours** to report damage or extra cleaning. Local Cooks reviews every report first; see the
  Damage & Cleaning Claims policy.

## 10. What kitchens agree to

- Keep each storage listing accurate: type, size, temperature, access hours and rules.
- Give access during the hours listed; keep cooler and freezer units at the stated temperature.
- Answer requests within 24 hours; use the tools in Local Cooks for unit changes, move-out dates and refunds.
- Take a unit **out of service** (for example a broken freezer) rather than leave a faulty unit rentable.
- Never charge chefs for storage outside Local Cooks.

**There is no deposit.**
