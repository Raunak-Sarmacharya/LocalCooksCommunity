# Rescheduling a kitchen booking: research and decision proposal

**Prepared / public sources checked: 2026-10-13. Design only; no implementation or account verification.** The latest owner decisions in `PHASE0_LOG.md`, including sections labelled Oct14, outrank older proposals in the three companion documents. Everything labelled **Recommend** below needs product approval; platform facts are separately identified. Source links are public documentation, not evidence of account-specific Stripe capabilities.

## 1. Decision in one minute

**Recommend a same-kitchen amendment, with an immutable proposal and separate financial adjustments.** Keep the original reservation until both parties agree and any extra payment succeeds. Preserve its booking reference, captured PaymentIntent and original receipt. Update its operational schedule atomically; never rewrite its captured payment. A decrease produces an auditable partial refund. An increase produces a new PaymentIntent for the difference. Show one booking with a change history and adjustment receipts.

The owner's premise is **financially correct but does not require cancel-and-rebook**: a captured payment cannot be increased; a new charge is necessary for extra money, not for every changed date. A same-price move needs no Stripe payment operation. Cancellation plus a full new booking creates another full processing fee, temporarily ties up more chef cash, can lose the replacement slot, and can unintentionally reset cancellation rights. Use that model for a different kitchen/payee, or an uncaptured request whose original checkout contract cannot be amended safely.

Pilot scope: future, unstarted, same-kitchen hourly/full-day bookings; manager approval; two successful chef-caused moves; self-service only before the original full-refund cutoff and at least 24 hours before both schedules. Whole unstarted multi-day ranges move together after the multi-day foundation ships. Support owns exceptions and every recovery state. In-session added time remains the **already-approved linked extension offer**, not a reschedule.

## 4. Recommended product contract

### Initiation, consent and limits

1. **Chef:** “Change date or time” proposes same kitchen and exact selected visits. Chef submission accepts the frozen quote/policy consequences. Manager approves/declines; price or schedule changes by manager require a new chef acceptance, never broader implied consent.
2. **Manager:** “Propose new time” is pre-approval for that exact proposal. Chef accepts/declines, including a $0 move. Manager must identify **optional accommodation** versus **kitchen cannot supply original**. Initiator and fault/cause are independent; a manager can enter a chef-requested move without making it kitchen-caused.
3. **Support:** creates/withdraws/extends/decides on verified instructions using the same commands, recording actor, acting-for party, reason and evidence. No consent inferred from silence. Support may grant a funded remedy within delegated authority; cannot override occupied inventory, legal access or a chef's refusal to pay.
4. **No automatic chef move in pilot**, even same kitchen, free slot and same price. Manager-approved original time does not imply staffing or equipment readiness at another time. Later opt-in auto-approval needs an explicit location default/kitchen override, audited eligibility and safe resource capacity; it is not included in v1.
5. **Self-service eligibility:** confirmed/paid; no started/consumed arrival, departure, inspection, unresolved dispute/refund or unidentified source allocation. Chef-caused moves finish before **both** current/new starts minus 24 h and before every affected unit's first full-refund cutoff. At an exact cutoff, preserve the policy's generous boundary; persist server receipt time, then enforce the accepted proposal deadline at commit. New time obeys current notice, duration, opening, horizon, blackout and turnover rules using location time zone/absolute instants.
6. **Two successful chef-caused moves per booking lifetime**, one open proposal at once; failed/declined requests do not consume the allowance. Rate-limit submissions separately to prevent hold spam. Kitchen-caused remedies do not count; support can grant a documented exception. Same-date time moves count. No retroactive “reschedule” of a used visit or no-show; evidence correction, compensation and a new booking remain available.
7. Proposal decision/consent deadline = min(submission + 24 h, applicable chef-move cutoff, earlier original/new start − preparation buffer). Use the adopted 30-min **proposed** buffer only after lifecycle configuration approval. Paid checkout uses existing 32-min expiry/37-min hold where enough time remains; otherwise use the supported shorter payment path or refuse checkout. Never create a Stripe Checkout expiry outside its supported range to squeeze under a deadline.
8. Pending request does not reserve the destination or change the original. Reserve replacement capacity only on manager approval or chef acceptance of a manager proposal, under authoritative kitchen/date/resource locks; display “subject to availability” earlier. A $0/decrease move revalidates and commits immediately. An increase holds replacement capacity briefly while payment completes; original remains reserved until commit.

**Access/document rule:** recommend treating replacement dates as a new scheduling commitment for eligibility: revoked access or expired required documents blocks the move until resolved through the application/renewal workflow. It does not revoke the existing booking, shorten its refund rights or bypass the locked protection for existing bookings. Same-kitchen amendments retain original accepted terms/preset; newly imposed financial policy or terms are not smuggled into the change. Safety/legal unavailability routes to a cause-specific support remedy.

### Captured payments, price and cancellation rights

**Recommend current destination kitchen price, with the original invitation discount explicitly retained for a like-for-like move; manager can improve the kitchen discount, not edit tax/platform fees.** Freeze quote components, currency, rate/discount provenance, expiry and both acceptances. Changed duration or hourly↔daily mode must be itemized; do not silently treat daily as hourly or reprice unaffected days. Minimum kitchen price remains the locked $1 floor. A tiny positive delta below Stripe's charge minimum needs a visible manager discount or support-funded waiver, never a rounded-up hidden charge.

- **Same final total:** no capture/refund/reversal; transfer/closeout timing may still change. Keep original captured ledger intact.
- **Price up:** collect only additional rent/equipment plus applicable tax and configured platform-fee difference through a new platform PaymentIntent; never add the original full payment again. Manager approval is recorded first. Recommend chef “Accept and pay $X” on-session with saved card, authentication/new-card fallback. Optional off-session charging after asynchronous approval requires explicit authorization of that exact quote and a mandate; authentication failure returns to the chef without confirming the move.
- **Price down:** before the self-service boundary, approve the full quoted price difference, including corresponding tax and returned platform-fee share. This is a price adjustment, not an ordinary cancellation; **no cancellation percentage or second processing-fee deduction**. Apply schedule plus durable refund obligation in one transaction, then execute/reconcile refund. The new schedule remains confirmed if refund takes time or fails; support owes the refund, not an itinerary rollback that steals the slot back.
- Recompute changed supplier lines and platform fees under their evidenced rules; reverse original tax allocations on removed value and calculate new supplied value under applicable tax rules. An accountant must approve credit-note/new-supply treatment for tax-rate changes; netting two gross totals is not sufficient accounting. Keep unchanged lines/independent storage intact. Fee=0 stays zero; do not introduce 7% from older documents.
- Actual processor fees on **each captured source** remain normal processing expenses in manager settlement; do not charge chefs an invented “reschedule fee”. Repricing downward can leave insufficient manager proceeds to cover already-incurred fees: manager consent to a funded discount/deficit, or approved platform subsidy, is required before commitment. No negative entitlement hidden in transfer math.
- Ordinary later chef cancellation uses the saved preset and remaining allocated pool: current carried value minus unreimbursed actual processor cost, with prior successful/pending refunds and paid adjustment sources accounted for once. Component/source allocations must reconcile; price refunds cannot be paid again as cancellation refunds. A platform-subsidized fee cannot also be withheld from the chef. Kitchen-caused cancellation returns remaining captured customer money in full; already-returned amounts are not refunded twice.

**Anti-gaming recommendation:** for each carried unit, keep original policy/terms version and `cancellationAnchorStart = min(all chef-caused accepted start instants)`. Cutoffs derive from this anchor, never a later replacement date; added payment for the same moved unit uses the same anchor. Moving earlier can bring cutoffs forward; moving later never restores a free/50% window. Display both actual visit time and actual cancellation deadlines before acceptance. Keep original booking/confirmation time; no new confirmation grace period. This follows the companion policy C25, not an asserted Airbnb standard.

Example: Flexible, original Fri 16 Oct 10:00, cutoff Thu 15 Oct 10:00. Move on Tue to Fri 23 Oct 10:00: refund cutoff stays Thu 15 Oct 10:00. Move instead to Thu 15 Oct 10:00: cutoff becomes Wed 14 Oct 10:00. A second move cannot undo that earlier anchor. Late cheaper moves could otherwise bypass retention; they are **support/manager exceptions**, with the original policy entitlement and any deliberate extra concession shown before consent.

**Kitchen-caused move:** the chef may keep original if supply is possible, accept a remedy, or take the locked full-refund kitchen cancellation. Never disguise inability to supply as chef cancellation. Recommend equivalent time at no higher chef cost; manager discounts additional rent, support separately funds any approved subsidy. Keep the original preset but reset deadlines to the replacement dates as a customer-favourable remedy, recording that exception. If chef declines or offer expires and original cannot be supplied, trigger kitchen cancellation; chef is not left liable for a fictitious available booking. Pilot processing-fee loss on kitchen cancellation belongs to Local Cooks (locked), not next-earnings recovery.

### Uncaptured requests, multi-day and adjacent offers

- **Pre-confirmation:** original request remains freely withdrawable. Recommend replacement checkout, not editing schedule under the original immutable Checkout metadata. Reserve proposed capacity, persist an exclusive amendment/decision token, and suppress manager capture of old request while replacement authorization runs. Explain temporary overlapping card holds. On verified new authorization and verified old release, replace the pending request; release old inventory and link the old/new records in the change history. Failure releases only replacement; original can continue only within its original deadline. Capture-winning-release races go to paid recovery, never a false “hold released” message.
- Do not restart the manager's original 24 h deadline through repeated edits. New deadline is bounded by original response deadline, new start buffer and actual new authorization expiry. If already expired, close old request truthfully and allow a separately initiated fresh request. Manager proposal is pre-approved only after chef accepts; a fresh authorized replacement can use the existing recorded confirmation/capture service, after old release is resolved.
- **Multi-day:** locked model is one booking + daily visits, per-day cancellation allocations, min/max consecutive-day constraints, default enabled for daily-rate kitchens (1–7 days), daily codes/tasks. Recommend whole-range moves before any day starts, same day count in pilot; preserve one-to-one per-day anchors/allocations. Check every new day atomically; one conflict refuses the range. No “skip closed days” or midnight-to-midnight access. Explicit hourly↔daily/range-size changes need a complete new quote and support in pilot.
- **Single-day changes within a range:** support-only for unstarted affected days; preserve used days, their evidence, money and claim deadlines. For a kitchen-caused closure, support can cancel/refund the affected day or replace it with an agreed future visit, with a documented exception to range continuity. No bulk reset of consumed days. Adding days is a separate booking/offer, not an unnoticed range amendment.
- **Equipment:** reselect/revalidate essential items for moved visits; show retained/replaced/removed items, quantity, dates and price/refund. Reuse line-item allocations, but resource capacity must be authoritative; existing equipment fulfillment is not proof of shared-item conflict safety. If equipment branch is unavailable, offer support/new booking with transparent money preview, not a kitchen-only move that drops an oven reservation.
- **Storage:** monthly independent contracts do not move, renew, refund or cancel with kitchen time. Retain legacy date/custody contracts as locked; show their unchanged dates and a separate “Manage my storage” link. Any required access/move-in change is its own agreement. Rescheduling never releases occupied storage, starts holdover or authorizes disposal.
- **Invites/extensions:** reuse offer UI, identity, quote/expiry and notifications. A manager amendment can be pre-approved like an invite, but a normal invitation's acceptance must not create an unrelated duplicate booking for carried funds. Approved adjacent early/late/extension time remains a linked paid booking displayed as one longer visit, retaining original payment. Withdraw unpaid extension offers when parent moves; an already-paid linked extension must move with the selected visit through one group proposal or receive its own consented cancellation/refund. Until group support exists, block self-service parent move and route to support.

## 5. Stripe mechanics: documented boundaries

- **Captured intent:** [increment API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment_intents/increment_authorization) requires eligible `requires_capture`; after capture it cannot be incremented. [Manual capture](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method) says most authorizations permit one capture; partial capture normally releases the balance. Use another PaymentIntent for additional captured money.
- **Saved/off-session:** [save-and-reuse](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/save-and-reuse?payment-ui=elements), [Setup Intents](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/setup-intents) require purpose-specific customer agreement. A new PaymentIntent can charge the saved platform Customer/payment method with `off_session` and confirmation; exemptions are not guaranteed. `authentication_required` needs the customer back on-session to authenticate the **same** intent. Do not create a second intent to bypass SCA.
- **Refunds:** [refund API](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds) supports partial/multiple refunds up to remaining captured amount; bank timing differs from provider success. Original processing fees generally are not returned under standard pricing. Reserve pending refunds in local caps; unknown provider result is not a failed refund.
- **Merchant/transfer separation:** [separate charges and transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers) refunds debit platform balance and do **not** reverse kitchen transfers. Reverse only the manager-funded portion of identified transfers, or reduce not-yet-transferred entitlement. Connected available balance/reversal limits may prevent recovery; platform can still owe the refund. A bank payout is not itself reversed by a transfer reversal.
- **Platform/application fees:** [Connect refunds](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/marketplace/tasks/refunds-disputes) documents `refund_application_fee` for actual application-fee arrangements. In Local Cooks' ordinary separate-charge model, fee is retained through transfer math; there is no application-fee object to refund. Return our owed service-fee allocation from platform balance and record it. Do not use destination-charge `reverse_transfer` as if it reverses separate transfers.
- **Authorization lifetime:** [validity table](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method#authorization-validity-windows) commonly gives online cards 7 days; Visa merchant-initiated authorizations can be about 5 days. Use charge `payment_method_details.card.capture_before`, not hardcoded seven days. Local Cooks' 24 h response deadline is an independent, shorter product clock.
- **Incremental authorization:** [availability](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/incremental-authorization?platform=web&ui=elements) depends on pricing/access, network/category and a completely uncaptured intent; standard-pricing access needs Stripe approval. It does not extend original authorization validity and does not solve a confirmed captured reschedule. Recommend no dependency on it or extended/multicapture features.

## 6. Minimal model and commit protocol

### Data: extend existing mechanism; no parallel reschedule table

Reuse `kitchen_booking_changes` with `kind='move'`, v3 policy/quote payloads, current state vocabulary and historical receipts. Keep v2 reconciliation dispatched separately. Proposed additive fields:

- Booking: `schedule_revision int default 1`, `reschedule_terms jsonb null` holding per-unit original/earliest anchor, policy-version references and explicit remedy overrides. Use the cancellation-policy engine's canonical snapshot instead if it already owns these fields at build time; do not create two policy authorities.
- Change: `initiated_by_user_id`, `initiator_role`, `acting_for_user_id` nullable, `cause` (`chef_request | kitchen_failure | support_remedy`), `applied_at` nullable, `operations jsonb` versioned and server-validated. Existing policy/history hold consent actors, quote hash, consent times, override funding, original/current manager/payee provenance. `operations` freezes each charge/refund/reversal/compensation leg, source IDs, amount, owner, durable operation key and verified receipt/status; one new charge, potentially several source-specific refunds.
- Existing original/destination JSON gain ordered `{visitId, unitKey, operatingDate, timezone, startInstant, endInstant, slots, pricingMode}` arrays. Equipment allocations and independent storage choices live in existing linked-items payload. No resetting history JSON on transitions; retain append-only events and receipt identifiers without card/code plaintext.
- Hold: reuse `kitchen_checkout_holds`, with an explicit change owner/reference and grouped per-day holds from the multi-day package. Existing single `holdId` can identify the lead/group in v3. Add only the ownership/group field missing after that package; all availability readers must recognize amendment holds and exclude only this booking/group's retained occupancy.
- Applied operational visit rows retain stable IDs for same units; save old schedule in change.original. Multi-day operating date comes from its prerequisite schema. No source/evidence row deletion. Maintain request uniqueness, one open change, provider uniqueness and a CAS on both booking schedule revision and proposal revision.

**Booking row choice:** mutate date/selected-slots/operational visits in place only after verified commit; keep original `paymentIntentId`, `paymentDecision`, capture amounts and receipts unchanged. Read current price from frozen applied quote + full payment/refund sources; never overwrite historical captured fields to pretend they equal the new price. All booking detail/list/earnings/receipt readers must distinguish original captured amount, revised contract total, paid balance and refund owed. One canonical projection is required; an old single-intent reader cannot silently survive the rollout.

Select the effective schedule/contract by `applied_at` and schedule revision, not just `state='applied'`: an applied decrease in `refund_pending` or recovery still governs the visit. Failed unapplied attempts never become current. Source refund status and operation state remain separate from schedule status.

Trade-off: one reference, conversation, add-on linkage and visit history is the least disruptive UX; report gross booking sales once, adjustments separately, revenue by **actual supplied dates**, cash by provider transaction dates. New linked replacement rows preserve original itinerary without mutation but require a new replacement relation, current-row selection, carried-credit ledger, cancellation lineage, payee/transfer allocation and duplicate-booking suppression. They are not simpler financially. Use linked rows for genuinely additional time/cross-kitchen replacement, not merely because capture occurred.

Show “Original time remains booked; Local Cooks is resolving the extra payment.” Refund failure after a committed decrease is instead “New time confirmed; refund needs attention.” Do not reuse one vague pending banner for both.

Same-day time changes invalidate codes too. Legacy code-free bookings remain legacy unless enrollment is explicitly selected under rollout rules; configured-code failures cannot silently downgrade enrolled visits to taps.

## 8. OWNER QUESTIONS — real product forks only

1. **Amend the same booking, or require cancellation + linked replacement for every paid move?** Recommend same-kitchen amendment with separate delta/refund ledger: one reference and no unnecessary full-charge fee/cash gap. Different kitchen remains a separately consented booking/remedy.
2. **Manager approval always, or automatic when same-kitchen inventory is free?** Recommend manager approval in pilot; empty slots do not prove staff/equipment readiness. Manager proposals already carry their approval, so chef acceptance needs no second manager click.
3. **Current destination price, or guarantee original rate?** Recommend current destination price with original invitation discount retained for a like-for-like move, previewed before consent; unaffected units stay at saved prices. Manager can improve rent discount. This supports demand/rate changes while honouring the promised discount.
4. **Carry original cancellation deadlines, or restart them at the new date?** Recommend earliest accepted start anchors for chef-caused moves, explicit before acceptance; kitchen-caused remedies get replacement-date deadlines under the original preset. Restarting chef deadlines enables refund-window gaming; keeping kitchen-failure deadlines penalizes the wrong party.
5. **How permissive should chef moves be?** Recommend two completed moves, only outside the original full-refund boundary and ≥24 h before both schedules; late moves and per-day/mid-range edits require support plus manager consent. This is conservative for production kitchens; allowing requests until start is a genuine alternative with more operational/support load.
6. **Cheaper move refunds and added processing cost: customer fee or marketplace/manager expense?** Recommend full agreed price difference, including tax/platform-fee adjustment, no new chef processing deduction; actual captured-source fees stay normal manager expense. Any negative manager entitlement requires explicit funded discount/subsidy approval. This is a proposed reschedule rule, not a change to locked ordinary cancellation fee retention.
