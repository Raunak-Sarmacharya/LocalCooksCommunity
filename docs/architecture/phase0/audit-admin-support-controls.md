# Local Cooks booking support controls: read-only audit

**Date:** 2026-10-12. **Scope:** repository evidence only. No database, Stripe, Vercel, or Inngest state was inspected or changed for this audit. “Exists” means a staff-facing route/UI exists in the reviewed code; it does not assert a successful live exercise. This is a design audit, not a claim that all enum combinations are reachable. The owner's D11 requires Local Cooks support to act for either party in every state; D4–D9 constrain money and timing (`docs/architecture/phase0/PHASE0_LOG.md:50-60`). The evidence pack records a successful sandbox approval/capture and cancellation/refund, but explicitly leaves manager decline, expiry, inside-window cancellation, storage, disputes, and payout failure unexercised (`docs/architecture/phase0/phase0-evidence-pack.md:33-44`).

## 1. State model support must see

Treat a case as a **product of independent state axes**, not one “booking status” badge. A staff action is allowed only after it reloads all affected axes and the provider evidence under a lock. The database booking status enum is `pending | confirmed | cancelled | completed | cancellation_requested`; payment status is `pending | authorized | processing | paid | refunded | failed | partially_refunded`, while the transaction ledger also has `succeeded | canceled` (`shared/schema.ts:26,56-65`). These are stored labels, not proof that Stripe settled or a bank received a refund (`server/services/booking-payment-decision.ts:126-169`; `client/src/components/admin/sections/AdminTransactionHistory.tsx:531-550`).

## 2. Present staff inventory and action matrix

The admin page mounts Transactions (including payment recovery), Email Log, Tours, damage review/history, overstay review/history, and historical visit review as separate sections (`client/src/pages/Admin.tsx:1138-1254`; `client/src/components/admin/sections/AdminTransactionHistory.tsx:1049,1231-1256`). Transaction search/filter exposes booking IDs, parties, type, status and Stripe identifiers (`server/routes/admin.ts:3330-3368`; `client/src/components/admin/sections/AdminTransactionHistory.tsx:1158-1189`). The booking support endpoint shows status, recorded decision, PaymentIntent and event history, but there is no single staff booking case screen (`server/routes/booking-attendance.ts:12-39`). “Missing” below is based on the reviewed staff routes and UI, not a claim about ad hoc Stripe Dashboard work.

**Common guards for every mutation:** authenticated named staff actor; select `acting_for = chef | manager | Local Cooks` and actual requester; reason code plus required internal note and evidence/reference; optimistic booking/transaction version; one immutable operation ID and provider idempotency key; journal intended and verified outcomes; recipient-specific notice to both sides (including if staff acted) via the durable outbox; fail closed on provider uncertainty. Read-only view-as must never turn a chef/manager URL or token into a staff mutation. The normal role routes require `requireChef` or `requireManager`, so support must use explicit staff service calls and retain the real staff identity (`server/routes/bookings.ts:2155,2296`; `server/routes/manager.ts:4642-4646`; `server/routes/kitchen-booking-changes.ts:37-51`).

### Funding rules that the UI must show before approval

1. **Uncaptured hold:** release, never call it a refund; manager gets no transfer and the chef incurs no booking charge (D4; `server/services/booking-payment-decision.ts:134-145,168-203`).
2. **Captured policy refund:** split customer amount among manager-share debit/transfer reversal and any authorized Local Cooks service-fee return. Processing cost stays with the allocation prescribed by D5/D6. Current staff full-refund path calculates `maxRefundable = min(captured - actualStripeFee - alreadyRefunded, remainingManagerShare + remainingServiceFee)` and records manager versus platform contributions (`server/routes/admin.ts:3646-3707`). This is an existing rule for that route, **not** a general goodwill facility.
3. **Goodwill:** choose explicit platform-funded expense if the manager has not agreed and no disclosed policy charges them. If customer cash exceeds the charge's remaining refundable amount, use a separately authorized credit or payment mechanism; never create a Stripe refund above the original capture. Whether Local Cooks may cover the actual Stripe fee is an owner policy question because D5/D6 currently prohibit refunding it.
4. **Separate charges/transfers:** a customer refund does not itself reverse a manager transfer. Reconcile the refund, transfer reversal, platform contribution and HST treatment as separate ledger entries before marking the case settled (`server/services/stripe-transfer-service.ts:7-28`; `server/services/stripe-service.ts:802-808`). Kitchen HST is manager-set and passed to the manager; Local Cooks HST on a positive service fee is its own supplier/tax treatment (D2/D3; `docs/architecture/phase0/PHASE0_LOG.md:51-52`; `docs/architecture/phase0/audit-copy-terminology.md:734-737`). Do not equate a Connect transfer with bank payout.

## 3. External benchmark and Stripe constraints

These are **public policy and help pages**, not evidence of internal staff permissions. Local Cooks should copy their explicit funding/consent boundaries, not their cancellation percentages.

| Marketplace | Publicly documented support pattern and implication |
| --- | --- |

## 4. Startup-sized delivery plan

**P0 — pilot gate.** Build one staff booking case from the current transaction/booking IDs, with role-specific read-only views, all linked items, booking/visit/cancellation/change/payment/transfer/claim/overstay/outbox overlays, current Stripe evidence and append-only notes. Expose explicit, reviewed actions: confirm/decline authorized request for manager; release hold for chef; replay recorded payment decision; cancel captured booking for either side under D5/D6 with a frozen quote and manager discretion; refund/reverse only that reserved operation; issue a capped platform-funded goodwill adjustment when authorized; correct attendance; retry original notice. For every other state, support can open a case, record either party's instruction, notify both, and route a guarded manual resolution to the named specialist; a missing button must not force staff to impersonate a member or edit a Stripe/DB record ad hoc. Flag unreconciled refunds, transfers, chargebacks and payout failures for that manual handling. Fix the D6 in-window block and D5 outside-window request behavior as prerequisite behavior, not a staff workaround (`server/routes/bookings.ts:2225-2280`; `docs/architecture/phase0/PHASE0_LOG.md:54-56`). Put a per-action before/after money preview and one-click link to the saved operation. Require a second staff approver for a proposed **CAD $100 or any action that makes Local Cooks' net contribution positive**, whichever occurs first; the actor cannot approve their own action. The owner must set the actual threshold and goodwill budget before pilot. Do not ship a generic raw amount/status editor.

**P1.** Add self-contained manager-funded discretionary partial refund controls and budget reporting for platform goodwill; support-led time changes and storage extensions, assisted visits/checkouts, claim and overstay adjustments, corrected-notice versioning, transfer correction with dual approval. Make any response-deadline adjustment explicitly conditional on Stripe authorization validity and both parties' notice.

**P2.** Cross-kitchen relocation, full Stripe dispute workspace, Connect payout-failure repair, bulk case analytics and safe self-serve support delegation. Do not use impersonation for mutations; the explicit actor/acting-for model should remain.

**Audit model (new `support_cases`, `support_actions`, `support_action_events`, append-only `support_notes`; existing lifecycle/payment histories remain linked evidence):** case ID, booking ID plus item/visit/transaction/claim/penalty IDs; real staff actor ID and role; acting-for party ID/role and verified request source (message/call/ticket ID); reason code, internal note, evidence IDs, policy snapshot/version and applicable clause; action type, requested amount and currency, manager debit, platform service-fee return, platform goodwill expense, processor fee, kitchen tax adjustment; precondition version and before-state JSON (selected non-secret fields); proposed after-state and quoted money; approval actor/time/threshold; immutable operation/idempotency key; Stripe PI/charge/refund/transfer/reversal/dispute IDs; state `proposed | awaiting_approval | executing | provider_pending | verified | failed | recovery_required | rejected`; timestamps and error; recipient notification event IDs and delivery status. Append every attempt, approval, provider response and correction. Never delete or silently rewrite an entry. Restrict attachments/PII and log read-only access separately. ## 5. Owner decisions required

1. When a manager cancels or cannot provide the kitchen, should Local Cooks cover the Stripe processing cost and give the customer the whole captured amount? D5/D6 currently say the cost is never refunded, while Airbnb/Peerspace/Turo host-cancellation policies often promise full customer refund. Define customer promise and funding separately.
2. What written evidence permits staff to debit a manager outside the disclosed policy: an in-app message, verified phone call, or explicit manager click? What is the maximum staff goodwill expense and second-approver threshold?

5. For manager-initiated cancellation, no-show, inaccessible kitchen, relocation and already-used add-ons, who pays any extra compensation and how is kitchen HST corrected? What evidence threshold governs staff decisions?
