- Ordinary confirmed cancellation is self-serve. Before the cutoff, the owner chose a refund excluding the actual Stripe processing cost, with disclosure. Inside the window, the saved kitchen policy determines entitlement; the manager may grant a discretionary extra refund excluding that processing cost. The manager cannot veto a policy-promised entitlement.
- The earlier platform-funded gross convenience-refund recommendation is not the selected voluntary-cancellation direction. A gross remedy for kitchen/platform non-delivery remains a recommendation requiring its own funding/contract decision; ordinary customer cancellation must not be silently applied to a locked or unavailable kitchen.
- Modular, connected kitchen terms are requested. Exact presets, tier percentages, equality boundaries, publication-lock transition and acknowledgement text remain design/approval work. Preserve the original proposal's interim active-booking edit lock until snapshot coverage and an approved transition protect existing bookings.

This direction does not authorize an indefinite claim-dependent hold, immediate release on early clearance, or a new settlement cohort before the provider/treasury/contract gates.
- Booking feedback is in the redesign scope and private for Local Cooks review, like tours. Participants may see their own submission. Other-party answers, conflict flags and ratings must not leak through participant APIs or public listings. A feedback disagreement is a support signal, not automatic proof of absence, damage or financial liability.
- Arrival/departure instructions must have a clear, prominent place distinct from actionable checklists/photos. Whether a tap or evidence item is required must reflect saved policy, with an assisted/offline exception. [Food Corridor sign-in/out](https://help.thefoodcorridor.com/en/articles/1414030-how-do-i-digitally-sign-in-out-at-my-kitchen), [Kitchen Door listing](https://help.thefoodcorridor.com/en/articles/1413900-create-your-listing-on-the-kitchen-door), [Airbnb arrival help](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3124), [Getaround mandatory inspections](https://getaround.com/help/articles/3726d00dbaa7/intl).
