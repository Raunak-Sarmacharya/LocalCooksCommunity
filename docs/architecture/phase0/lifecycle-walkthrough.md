## How a booking ends today (code, verified)
- `completed` is set **only** when every visit is `checked_out`/`checkout_claim_filed`: the manager clears the inspection, or it auto-clears 60 min after the chef requests checkout (`kitchen-visit-lifecycle.ts:36-47,242-266`; window `kitchen_checkout_review_window_minutes` = 60, admin-only).
- If the chef never taps check-in/check-out, the booking stays **"Confirmed · Not Checked In" forever**. No-show detection is deliberately off (`detectKitchenVisitNoShows` returns 0). After 48 h (`historical_visit_review_after_hours`) it appears in a Local Cooks historical-review queue, where staff record attended/no-show, but that records attendance only (no completion, no money).
- Nothing financial happens at the end: the manager was paid at confirmation.
- Check-in/out time maths are hard-coded to `America/St_Johns` (`kitchen-visit-lifecycle.ts:81,115,127`) even though the location's zone is loaded.
- No post-booking reviews or ratings exist.

## Money: when does the manager get paid? (core structural decision)
Today: **transfer at capture (confirmation)**, then Stripe pays out to the bank on the account schedule. So for a booking made 2+ weeks ahead, the money is in the manager's bank before the session. Consequences:
1. **Every post-confirmation cancellation needs a transfer reversal.** If the manager has already been paid out, the reversal fails (insufficient balance) and, because `losses.payments = application`, **Local Cooks absorbs the loss**.
2. Kitchen no-shows or "kitchen unavailable" can't be remedied from held funds.
3. Damage/overstay claims have no settlement to offset against.

Top marketplaces pay **after the service starts or ends**: [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/20) ~24 h after check-in; [Peerspace](https://intercom.help/peerspace-108cf19aff8e/en/articles/10119141) within 7 days after the reservation ends; [Turo](https://help.turo.com/en_us/getting-paid-SyALrVeN9.md) ~3 h after the trip ends (3 business days for a first trip).

**Recommendation (startup-sized):** transfer to the manager **after the scheduled session end + a short closeout window** (e.g. 24 h, or immediately when the manager clears checkout), not at confirmation. Funds wait on the Local Cooks platform balance with `source_transaction`, inside Stripe's holding limits. This removes most reversals, makes cancellations and kitchen-fault remedies simple refunds, and gives claims a place to land. Trade-off: managers wait ~1 day after the session instead of being paid up front, which is the norm on every comparable marketplace. Phase 5 of `booking-target-phases.md` already designs this ("deferred settlement B"); the live evidence above is the reason to adopt it.

## Target lifecycle (proposal)
`requested (hold)` → `confirmed (captured)` → `in progress` (start reached; check-in optional evidence) → `ended` (scheduled end reached, automatic) → `closed` (manager cleared, or auto after closeout window, or open case) → `settled` (transfer sent) → `paid out` (bank, from Stripe webhook).
Cancellation/no-show/claim are overlays with their own state, never a reason a booking stays "Confirmed" forever. Attendance (check-in/out) is evidence, not the clock: the clock is the scheduled time.

## Open decisions surfaced by this run
1. Adopt deferred settlement (transfer after session + closeout) instead of at confirmation? (Recommended.)
2. Bookings made inside the penalty window: block, warn and require acknowledgement, or add a short grace (e.g. free cancel within 1 h of booking if the start is > X h away)?
3. Auto-complete at scheduled end (+ grace) regardless of check-in? (Recommended.)
4. Post-booking reviews: in scope for the redesign or later?

### Research qualifications

Airbnb's ordinary payout is around check-in, not after inspection. Peerspace pays after the event and treats later extra charges separately; Turo's US payout is typically three hours after subsequent trips, and Getaround's cited international schedule includes rentals ended at least 48 hours before transfer date. These support a bounded post-service product choice, not a universal payout standard. See cited sources in `design-checkin-checkout.md` and `continuation-handoff.md`.

Mandatory vehicle inspection in Getaround and PIN clocking in Food Corridor do not establish a universal mandatory app tap for kitchens. Guidance should make arrival/departure clear; missed/offline taps need an assisted, time-bounded exit. Instructions, attendance evidence, service end, inspection, case, transfer and bank payout must stay distinguishable.
