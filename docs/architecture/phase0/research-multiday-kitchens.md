# Multi-day and repeat commercial-kitchen bookings

Researched 2026-10-17. Scope: public first-party documentation and operators' own pages; no authenticated bookings or code inspection. Pages cited below were fetched, rather than relying on search snippets. This is an evidence sample, not a market-share ranking.

**Labels:** DOCUMENTED = software provider explicitly describes it; OPERATOR = kitchen describes its own practice; INFERRED = interpretation or recommendation, not a verified feature. **Not verified** means no supporting public evidence was retrieved; it does not mean unsupported. An operator's practice is not automatically a platform default.

**Reading the tables:** dates/hours answers questions 1–2; grouping/approval answers 3; controls answers 4; changes answers 5; billing/payout/extras answers 6–7. Unless specified, invoice count, series approval granularity, maximum duration/occurrences, blackouts, member limits, per-occurrence cancellation mechanics, and payout timing are **not verified**. Monthly membership is not evidence of a multi-date checkout. A day/week rental rate is not evidence of arbitrary-date selection.

## Patterns most kitchen platforms share

These are **INFERRED cross-source patterns in this sample**, not claims that a majority of the entire market has been measured.

- **Separate visits remain meaningful even when entered as a repeat.** TFC supports individual removal and per-visit billing; Skedda supports occurrence exceptions and separate lock-in times. This supports a series/group over visits rather than one uninterrupted occupancy interval. [TFC admin][tfc-admin], [TFC hourly billing][tfc-hourly], [Skedda lock-in][sk-lock].
- **Repeat production is often sold through membership as well as scheduling.** Hour/credit packages, reserved weekly blocks, and monthly equipment-day allowances recur across TFC, PREP, Magpie, and Union Kitchen. A membership invoice and a booking series solve different problems. [TFC plans][tfc-plans], [PREP][prep], [Magpie][magpie], [Union Kitchen][union].
- **Kitchen and equipment availability need separate treatment.** TFC supports equipment sub-times, PREP dedicated members still reserve equipment, and La Cocina distinguishes reservable equipment from first-come smallwares. [TFC booking][tfc-book], [PREP][prep], [La Cocina][lacocina].
- **Operators retain substantial control.** Approval/client privileges, availability windows, production limits, and priority access vary with the operator/member relationship. [TFC approvals][tfc-approve], [Skedda conditions][sk-conditions], [La Cocina][lacocina], [MiFoodStudio][mi].
- **No fetched source establishes arbitrary chosen dates with different daily hours in one renter checkout as a universal convention.** Strongest documented software precedent is a repeating template with occurrence management; Canadian standing schedules can also be staff-arranged. This is a limit of the evidence, not proof the feature is absent. [TFC booking][tfc-book], [Skedda repeats][sk-repeat], [MiFoodStudio][mi].

## Where they differ

- **DOCUMENTED/OPERATOR — payment timing:** TFC hourly charges follow each visit; Skedda can collect the whole series upfront; Hamlet consolidates member usage monthly; Nimbus distinguishes hourly pay-after-shift from temporary blocks paid at booking. [TFC][tfc-hourly], [Skedda][sk-repeat], [Hamlet][hamlet], [Nimbus][nimbus].
- **DOCUMENTED/OPERATOR — cancellation is not standardized:** TFC permits configured late fees; Skedda locks self-service actions per occurrence unless upfront payment locks the series; La Cocina advertises a 24h cutoff and 5% late fee; Magpie makes confirmed single shifts final. [TFC][tfc-cancel], [Skedda][sk-lock], [La Cocina][lacocina], [Magpie][magpie-cancel].
- **OPERATOR — standing access versus flexible shifts:** One World preserves regular schedules while eligibility continues; Magpie holds weekly blocks under monthly agreements; Nimbus hourly members self-book first-come shifts up to 60 days ahead, with 2h prep/4h standard blocks and changes free until six days ahead. Nimbus temporary access can span up to 14 days, is staff-booked, paid at booking, and permits no changes/cancellations. [One World][ow-faq], [Magpie][magpie], [Nimbus LES][nimbus].
- **DOCUMENTED/OPERATOR — booking access is not always approval per visit:** TFC allows per-client auto-approval; KitchenShare.AI advertises host review of each booking; other operators arrange production schedules directly. Do not assume a platform-wide approval model from “book online.” [TFC][tfc-approve], [KitchenShare.AI][ks-ai], [U-Cook][ucook].
- **DOCUMENTED — payout evidence is sparse:** TFC documents settlement by processed transaction and business-day bank delays. None of the other fetched sources establishes a marketplace guarantee equivalent to Local Cooks' approximately 24h-after-visit kitchen payout. [TFC transfers][tfc-payout].

## Recommended for Local Cooks with reasons

Local Cooks baseline below is supplied task context, not independently verified implementation: approved chefs; hourly/full-day pricing plus equipment; request + card authorization; acceptance within 24h then capture; kitchen chooses 24/48/72h notice and 50%/100% late charge; payout approximately 24h after each visit. The following are **INFERRED recommendations**, not competitor capabilities.

1. **One request group containing independently dated visits.** Let chefs choose consecutive or nonconsecutive dates, apply common hours/full-day to all, then override individual days and equipment. Show each date, cost, equipment availability, and cancellation cutoff before submission. This extends the documented occurrence model while meeting a flexibility need not established as a standard competitor checkout feature. [TFC individual edits][tfc-edit], [Skedda occurrence exceptions][sk-repeat].
2. **Start with one kitchen and bounded groups; add weekly recurrence as a convenience.** Expand daily/weekly repeats into a reviewable list with an explicit end date or count. Proposed launch defaults: maximum 14 visits and 90-day horizon, configurable later; these numbers are product choices, not researched norms. Validate every visit against availability, equipment capacity, duration rules, and horizon. Explain conflicts and require explicit removal/reselection rather than silently skipping dates. [TFC horizon enforcement][tfc-window], [Skedda repeat validation][sk-repeat].
3. **One all-or-nothing kitchen decision within the existing 24h window.** Present all dates in one approval view; kitchen accepts/declines the group. Store each occurrence independently after acceptance. Defer partial acceptance until chefs can explicitly approve a revised list and price. This is a simplicity choice; a shared-kitchen industry standard for atomic series approval was not verified. Use time-limited inventory reservations and recheck all slots before acceptance. [TFC approval precedent][tfc-approve], [Skedda contrasting nonblocking requests][sk-approval].
4. **Preserve capture on acceptance for the initial multi-date release.** Authorize the clearly disclosed group total at submission; capture once on acceptance, allocate amounts to each visit for refunds and kitchen transfers, and release authorization on rejection/expiry. This keeps current payment semantics concrete; it means all accepted days are paid upfront, which must be explicit. Whole-series upfront payment has a documented precedent. [Skedda][sk-repeat]. **DOCUMENTED technical constraint:** authorizations expire and most Stripe payments allow only one capture; inspect `capture_before`, rather than assuming a hold can last until the final visit or be captured once per future visit. [Stripe authorization/capture](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).
5. **Treat distant recurring production as a later billing product.** If total upfront cost becomes impractical, offer explicit per-occurrence charging with saved-payment consent, failure handling, and clear unsecured-future-visit status. Do not describe a saved card as guaranteed future funds. This would change today's capture-on-acceptance model and needs a separate product decision; TFC's pay-after-visit model is a precedent, not a mandate. [TFC hourly billing][tfc-hourly], [Stripe capture limitations](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).
6. **Calculate cancellation separately for every visit, even for “cancel remaining.”** Freeze the selected kitchen policy and daily price on acceptance. Compute each cutoff from that visit's start in the kitchen's timezone; at or before the exact cutoff, no late fee. After cutoff, retain 50%/100% of that visit's disclosed cancellation basis and refund its remainder. Explicitly state whether equipment is included. Show an itemized bulk-cancel preview; never apply the first visit's deadline to later days. [TFC fee basis][tfc-cancel], [Skedda per-occurrence lock-in][sk-lock].
7. **Offer “this day” and “all remaining days” actions.** Route date/time/equipment changes through kitchen review; keep the original visit until the replacement is accepted. Within the cancellation window, prevent moving/shortening a visit from bypassing the fee. Completed/cancelled visits stay in history; future visits remain usable after one cancellation. [TFC change-request behavior][tfc-edit], [TFC admin series removal][tfc-admin].
8. **Keep payout and accounting at visit level.** Release only the completed visit's allocated kitchen proceeds on the existing approximately 24h schedule; keep later visits pending. Track per-day tax, add-ons, refunds, fees, and transfer status, even with one payment and one group receipt. Define how late-cancellation proceeds are released. This preserves the supplied payout promise; competitors did not establish it as their norm. [Contrasting TFC settlement][tfc-payout], [Skedda single series charge][sk-repeat].
9. **Add “book again” before memberships, waitlists, and complex discounts.** Copy selected past dates' hours/equipment into a new request, require new dates, recheck availability, and reprice before authorization. This is an original low-complexity convenience proposal, not a verified competitor feature. Later consider monthly-hour packages or guaranteed standing blocks if demand justifies separate billing/priority rules. [TFC plan precedent][tfc-plans], [Magpie standing blocks][magpie], [PREP monthly hours][prep].
