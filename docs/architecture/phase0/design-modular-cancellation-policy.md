# Phase 0 design: modular kitchen cancellation policy

**Status:** design for owner review, 2026-10-12. No implementation or payment-account changes. The six owner decisions in the brief govern this design. References to current behavior below are source observations, not proposed behavior. Currency is CAD and all calculation inputs are integer cents.

## 1. Decision and evidence

### Product contract

1. A request awaiting manager confirmation has an **uncaptured card authorization**. The person who booked can withdraw instantly; cancel the authorization and release the whole hold. No charge, refund, or processing-fee deduction is made.
2. Once confirmed and captured, cancellation is always effective immediately. At or before the start of the kitchen's first penalty tier, the automatic refund is **the captured amount less the actual Stripe card-processing fee for that payment**. The Local Cooks service fee and its HST, if charged, are included in the refundable amount. Never describe this as a “free” or “100%” refund. At the cancel confirmation screen show the exact captured amount, exact fee, exact refund, and retained amount before the action is submitted.
3. Later, the frozen tier's percentage is automatic; the terminal default tier is 0%. The manager can grant an additional refund after cancellation, bringing cumulative customer refunds up to, but never above, captured amount minus the same actual processing fee. An already promised policy refund has no manager approval gate. An extra manager award is funded by reversing the manager's transfer for its manager-funded share; any returned Local Cooks fee and associated HST comes from Local Cooks' balance. “Manager can refund up to the cap” describes the customer-facing total, not an assertion that the whole amount exists in the manager transfer.
4. No booking damage deposit is offered or collected. Remove deposit settings and promises from the booking path, including stale legal/customer copy. Damage claims, if retained, are a separate evidence and consent workflow.
5. Only the frozen structured policy can determine a cancellation penalty. Supplemental uploaded kitchen terms may add house rules but cannot reduce a structured refund, impose a new cancellation charge, or override platform remedies. Later policy or upload edits affect new payments only.
6. The kitchen's HST rate is manager-supplied and the kitchen HST collected at checkout belongs in the manager's gross. Local Cooks is GST/HST registered: when its service fee is positive, calculate and separately disclose the applicable HST on that fee. At a configured 0% service fee, omit the fee **and its HST** from every user-facing surface.

**Important boundary:** the processing-fee deduction above applies to a *chef-initiated convenience cancellation of a supplied booking*. It is not a remedy for manager cancellation, lack of access, unsafe or materially misdescribed premises, a duplicate charge, or a mandatory statutory/card remedy. For those cases refund the full amount paid; Local Cooks funds the Stripe cost if the manager's transfer alone cannot. Counsel and the accountant must sign off on this exception hierarchy and tax treatment before public launch. It preserves the owner's prohibition on making the manager *refund the Stripe fee* through a discretionary cancellation refund.

### What the sources establish, and what they do not

- **Marketplace pattern.** [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/4052) shows the listing's policy on the listing, at payment, and in the confirmation email; it previews the refund before cancellation, and [returns its service fee in a free-cancellation period](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3066). [Vrbo](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/What-are-the-cancellation-policy-options) offers tiered listing policies, preserves the booked policy after edits, gives its displayed policy precedence over conflicting rental-agreement text, and requires a custom refund to meet the policy minimum. [Peerspace](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119471-what-is-the-cancellation-and-refund-policy) publishes tier tables that distinguish guest refund from host payout and sometimes deducts processing fees; it also [releases the authorization on a declined request](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119524-what-happens-when-i-receive-a-booking-request). [Turo](https://turo.com/us/en/policies/cancellation) says guest cancellation is effective immediately and the refund varies by timing and circumstance. [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/content/terms.html) says supplier cancellation terms are shown on supplier pages, during booking, and in confirmation, and determine cancellation/no-show refunds. [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.ca/help/en-us/articles/130304/how-to-set-a-refund-policy/) permits organizer policies and automated eligible refunds, with a choice of who bears ticketing fees; its [minimum policy rules](https://www.eventbrite.ca/help/en-ca/articles/827759/eventbrites-organizer-refund-policy-requirements/) require posted terms and prohibit retroactive worsening. These are product comparators, not legal approval for this marketplace or a claim that their processor economics match ours.
- **Stripe cost.** [Stripe Canada pricing](https://stripe.com/en-ca/pricing) says original processing, Connect, and currency conversion fees are not returned on standard card refunds. This establishes a merchant expense; it **does not** require or itself authorize charging that expense to the cardholder. [Stripe balance transactions](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance_transactions/object) expose `fee` and `fee_details` on the charge balance transaction. The owner selected a narrower deduction, **actual card-processing fee**: exclude Connect, FX, Radar, tax on Stripe services, refund fees, and any markup. If Stripe's line items or account pricing cannot isolate it reliably, do not substitute an estimated rate or the aggregate `balance_transaction.fee`; hold the refund quote for reconciliation and show a support state. Confirm the exact classification with Stripe for this account.
- **Card-not-present acceptance.** [Visa public rules, §5.4.2.5, pp. 382–383](https://cis.visa.com/dam/VCOM/download/about-visa/visa-rules-public.pdf) require a restricted cancellation/refund policy to be clearly disclosed in the e-commerce page sequence before final checkout with a click-to-accept acknowledgement; a linked policy can be part of that acknowledgement. [Mastercard transaction rules, §§3.11–3.11.1, p. 111](https://www.mastercard.com/content/dam/public/mastercardcom/na/global-site/documents/transaction-processing-rules.pdf) require sufficiently prominent, clear transaction terms before completion and permit electronic acceptance. Mastercard's [chargeback guide](https://www.mastercard.com/content/dam/mccom/shared/business/support/rules-pdfs/chargeback-guide.pdf) specifically discusses undisclosed refund limitations. A generic terms link or post-payment email is insufficient evidence of this particular deduction. The public Mastercard file served during research is dated 10 June 2025; ask the acquirer to confirm its current Canadian rules.
- **Canadian price and customer law.** The [Competition Bureau's drip-pricing guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/drip-pricing) says an advertised price that cannot be attained because of obligatory fees is deceptive, with the narrow government-imposed-charge exception; [general-impression guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations-and-deceptive-marketing-practices) covers materially misleading product and business-interest claims. This is relevant even where the kitchen renter uses the premises for business: do not assume that “B2B” makes a misleading public price or “full refund” claim safe. Newfoundland and Labrador's [Consumer Protection and Business Practices Act, ss. 2–3, 28–33](https://www.assembly.nl.ca/legislation/sr/statutes/c31-1.htm) excludes a person acting for business purposes from its definition of *consumer*; a sole proprietor's actual purpose, not job title alone, controls. Its no-deduction refund rule for certain statutory distance-sale cancellations is **not** a general convenience-cancellation rule. Other provinces and mixed-purpose bookings require a jurisdiction-specific review; the structured policy cannot waive non-excludable rights.

**Assessment of decision 2:** technically feasible **only after** the captured charge's processing-fee component is known and verified. It is weaker for customer trust than Airbnb-style full refunds and is sensitive to card type; the previous research memo recommended Local Cooks absorb it. The owner's decision is implementable as a clearly accepted, actual-cost-only cancellation deduction, subject to written legal/acquirer and tax review. Avoid any “full refund,” “free cancellation,” or “100% back” label for a captured payment. Do not infer from Stripe's refusal to return its fee that retaining it from the customer is automatically enforceable.

**Exact pre-payment disclosure, beside the price and linked policy:**

> If you cancel before your booking is confirmed, we release the hold on your card and you will not be charged. After confirmation, if you cancel by {fullRefundDeadlineLocal}, we refund your payment **minus the actual Stripe card-processing fee charged for this payment**. Stripe does not return that fee to us. The exact fee depends on your card and will be shown in dollars before you confirm a cancellation. Any Local Cooks service fee charged for this booking is refunded during this period. From {penaltyWindowStartLocal}, {kitchenName}'s cancellation terms apply: {generatedTierSummary}. If the kitchen cannot provide your booking, a different remedy may apply.

**Exact review-step acknowledgement, unchecked by default and logged:**

> I have reviewed {kitchenName}'s cancellation terms and understand that, after confirmation, a cancellation refund excludes the actual Stripe card-processing fee for my payment. I will see the exact amount before I cancel. Before confirmation, cancelling only releases my card hold.

The time placeholders include date, time, and kitchen time-zone abbreviation. In the pilot, do not promise a pre-payment dollar estimate as the actual fee; an optional example must be labelled as an example and show the card-type variability. The fee must not be added to the checkout charge as a second line item or described as a Stripe-imposed customer fee.

## 3. Policy model and publication rules

### Tables and immutable shape (proposed)

`kitchen_cancellation_policies`: `id`, `kitchen_id`, `version` (integer unique per kitchen), `status` (`draft|published|retired`), `preset_origin` (`flexible|moderate|strict|custom`), `schema_version`, `tiers_json`, `extra_terms_asset_id` nullable, `extra_terms_sha256` nullable, `rendered_en_ca` plus versioned translations, `effective_for_new_payments_at`, `created_at`, `published_at`, `published_by`. A published row is append-only; editing clones a draft and publishing assigns the next version. A kitchen has one active published version. A signed/hash-addressed upload is immutable; replacing the file creates a new asset/version. Store the original bytes in restricted durable storage and expose a durable view link, not a mutable URL alone.

Tier schema example (sorted by decreasing `minHoursBeforeStart`):

```json
{
  "schemaVersion": 1,
  "timeBasis": "elapsed_hours_before_each_session_start",
  "tiers": [
    { "minHoursBeforeStart": 72, "refundPercent": 100 },
    { "minHoursBeforeStart": 24, "refundPercent": 50 },
    { "minHoursBeforeStart": 0, "refundPercent": 0 }
  ]
}
```

Evaluate the first tier for which `cancellationReceivedAt <= sessionStartAt - minHoursBeforeStart × 3,600,000 ms`; equivalently, `remainingMs >= thresholdMs`. Thus at **exactly** 72 hours Moderate is 100%; one millisecond later it is 50%; at exactly 24 hours it is 50%; one millisecond later it is 0%. A terminal `0` tier covers cancellation before the start; at or after start, attendance/use and remedy rules take over. Evaluate each not-yet-started session separately if a booking spans distinct days. Do not silently apply a kitchen-session policy to already-used storage or separate charges.

**Presets are editable templates, not opaque enums:** Flexible `[24h→100%, 0h→0%]`; Moderate `[72h→100%, 24h→50%, 0h→0%]`; proposed Strict `[168h→100%, 72h→50%, 0h→0%]`, behind owner approval before exposing it. “100%” in editor means **100% of the refundable pool after the one actual processing-fee deduction**, and generated customer text must say that explicitly. Default a new kitchen to Flexible. No grace period is silently imported from another marketplace.

**Proposed guardrails, to confirm with owner:** threshold integer elapsed hours `0..720` (30 days); 2–5 tiers; unique descending thresholds; required final `0h` tier; earliest tier must be 100% and at least 24 hours before start; each later tier's percentage integer `0..100` and non-increasing toward start; terminal default 0%; no negative or >100 values, no gaps, duplicate cutoffs, NaN, conflicting locale text, or unbounded “no refunds ever” preset. Manager can be more generous (including 100% at 0h), and can grant later discretionary relief, but cannot remove the preconfirmation hold-release floor, postpone an automatic refund, or demand a fee beyond the published terms. The platform's **maximum penalty-window length** is 720 hours by this proposal. If a booking is made/confirmed inside a penalty tier, show its immediately applicable tier before payment. For bookings too close to start to have any full-refund period, the owner must decide whether to permit them, add a short confirmation grace, or block them.

Supplemental text/upload editor: show a required declaration that it adds operational terms only. Parse/search for cancellation, refund, deposit, no-show and payment clauses; flag for human review. Machine scanning is a backstop, not proof of compatibility. Publish only after reviewer checks against the structured version. On conflict, show and enforce mandatory law/platform remedies, then the frozen structured version, then consistent supplemental terms; a contradictory PDF clause cannot be used to keep funds. The generated prose is a pure function of the versioned data plus booking-specific deadlines, never a free-text manager interpretation.

### Payment-time booking snapshot

At creation of the Checkout quote/authorization, persist an immutable `booking_policy_acceptances` record keyed by Checkout Session/PaymentIntent (because `server/services/stripe-checkout-service.ts:105` creates Checkout before the booking), then link it to `kitchen_bookings` on fulfillment. Fields: policy ID/version/schema, exact tiers JSON, generated prose/locale/version, attachment asset ID/hash, kitchen time zone, each session's UTC start and computed UTC deadlines plus displayed local deadlines, acceptance timestamp, authenticated user/cardholder identity and checkout session, disclosure text version/hash, total/card currency, component amount cents (kitchen, equipment, storage, kitchen HST, Local Cooks fee, fee HST), supplier/tax identity and tax rates, refund-cost rule version. `kitchen_bookings` stores `policy_snapshot_id` (or immutable JSON plus hash), `policy_accepted_at`, and `cancellation_effective_at`; `payment_transactions` stores `charge_balance_transaction_id`, verified `card_processing_fee_cents`, fee provenance/classification, and cumulative per-source refund allocations. The snapshot must be assigned **at payment authorization**, not re-read from manager settings when the booking is created or confirmed. Hash the canonical snapshot and record it in the payment metadata if size permits; keep the full version in the database.

Time is an instant: use `timestamptz` for acceptance/cancellation/deadline and IANA kitchen zone only for display. Existing `timestamp without time zone` values are mixed across hosts per the evidence pack; do not bulk convert without per-row provenance. For changed booking times, preserve the original accepted policy version and obtain explicit acceptance of new deadline/price if the amendment changes the cancellation result. No edit resets a grace period unless separately approved.

## 4. Refund calculation and money movement

### Pure quote specification

Input: immutable booking snapshot, cancellation received UTC instant, source-specific captured charge/transfer facts, verified original card-processing fee `F`, successful prior refund/reversal receipts, and optional manager additional target. Output: tier, policy-owed refund, cumulative maximum, incremental customer refund, manager transfer reversal, Local Cooks-funded return, retained customer amount, tax-component allocations, audit reason and stable quote hash. Quote and cancel use the **same server function**; client arithmetic is display only. A quote has a short validity interval and must be re-evaluated under the booking/source lock at submission. The cancellation instant saved on first accepted request fixes the tier even if Stripe execution completes after a cutoff.

For each captured card charge: `T = captured`, `K = kitchen gross including manager-set HST`, `P = Local Cooks fee plus its HST`, require `T = K + P`, `0 <= F <= K`, and `M = K - F` (original manager-transfer entitlement). The cumulative chef convenience-cancellation refund cap is `C = T - F = M + P`. For tier percentage `p`, cumulative policy entitlement is `E = roundHalfUp(C × p/100)`. Subtract prior successful refunds attributable to that source to obtain the *incremental* refund; never refund twice. For a manager's extra award, choose a cumulative customer total `D` with `E <= D <= C` and `D >= alreadyRefunded`. The manager-funded cumulative share at target `D` is `roundHalfUp(D × M/C)` capped to `[0,M]`; the Local Cooks-funded share is the exact residual `D - managerShare` (largest-remainder equivalent). Incremental reversals and platform returns subtract already verified funding shares. At `C=0`, no paid refund can be quoted. The allocation rule returns cents exactly and makes a 100% target reverse `M` while returning `P` from Local Cooks. The manager's extra choice is shown as the **customer refund total**; the dialog separately shows its manager-transfer reversal.

Use exact decimal/integer arithmetic with half-up at the cent only at defined boundaries; never binary floating-point `Math.round` for a financial contract. Calculate original kitchen HST and fee HST separately from their respective tax bases using accepted rates and freeze charged amounts. For a partial refund, allocate tax-related cash within each supplier component by a deterministic proportional/largest-remainder method so displayed line items sum to the exact Stripe refund. **Accounting/tax caveat:** the unrecovered processor fee and any retained cancellation amount may be consideration/forfeiture requiring a different GST/HST treatment from the original rental. Do not automatically issue a “100% HST credit” because a customer refund says “100% of refundable pool.” The accountant must approve credit-note, cancellation-payment and remittance rules under [CRA GST/HST special cases](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-special-cases.html) and [Excise Tax Act s. 182](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-15/section-182.html). Never move the kitchen's HST to Local Cooks revenue.

The illustrative `$3.64` and `$3.87` values are *assumed verified provider facts*, not a promise derived from 2.9% + 30¢. The fee can change with card type and account agreement. For multiple captured sources or independently priced add-ons, compute each source's fee, cap, tier applicability and receipts separately, then sum; never deduct one fixed processing fee twice or apply a kitchen tier to a used storage period without its own accepted terms. If an add-on shares the original charge, use frozen component allocations; `server/services/booking-cancellation-refund.ts:95-114` already treats ambiguous bundled allocations as review-required.

**Fee provenance and unknown fee:** fetch the captured Charge's `balance_transaction` through the PaymentIntent; store its ID/currency, `fee_details`, classification and exact card-processing component. Current `server/services/stripe-service.ts:301-311` reads `balance.fee`, which can be broader than the owner's rule. Do not use `platform_settings` estimate (`shared/booking-pricing-breakdown.ts:164-196`) or predicted domestic-card price. On a still-authorized booking `F=0` by definition of no charge. On a captured booking whose actual component is not yet known, allow **instant cancellation**, persist the received time and frozen entitlement, block new payout, and show “Your booking is cancelled. We’re confirming the card-processing fee before showing and issuing the exact refund.” Retry on `charge.updated`/reconciliation. If the UI cannot show exact dollars, it must **not** present a paid-cancellation confirmation action with an estimated amount; support an alternative cancellation submission that records the time and explicitly says the quote is pending, then notify the customer of the final amount. This is an unavoidable operational edge case under the owner's simultaneous instant-cancel and exact-preview requirements; owner must approve the wording/flow. Never let the fee delay push the customer into a worse tier.

### Provider operations by scenario

| Scenario | Booking action | Stripe and ledger action |
|---|---|---|
| Pending, uncaptured | Cancel immediately, release inventory | Cancel PaymentIntent in `requires_capture` or expire Checkout as appropriate. No refund or transfer reversal; mark hold-release requested/verified separately from bank display timing. [Stripe PaymentIntent cancel](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment_intents/cancel). |
| Confirmed, before first penalty cutoff | Cancel immediately at saved receipt time | Reserve refund operation `C`; reverse actual manager transfer `M` (or reduce the unpaid transfer entitlement if transfer not yet sent); refund `C` on platform charge; Local Cooks funds `P`. Record Stripe refund and reversal IDs, success/pending status, exact fee retained. |
| Confirmed, partial or 0% tier | Cancel immediately | Reserve `E`; if positive, reverse manager share and refund corresponding platform share. At 0%, no Stripe refund/reversal; money remains per disclosed tier. |
| Manager adds relief after cancellation | Booking remains cancelled | Set cumulative `D` between policy entitlement and `C`; issue only `D - successfulRefunds`. Reverse incremental manager share, return incremental platform share. Never allow a manager entry that reduces an already owed or paid amount. |
| Manager/platform failure or mandatory remedy | Cancel or mark failed supply with separate reason | Refund `T` including processor-cost equivalent to the customer; reverse manager entitlement, Local Cooks funds the gap `P+F` unless a separately agreed manager liability mechanism applies. This is **not** the chef convenience-cancellation cap. |

Separate charges and transfers have **no automatic refund-driven transfer reversal**: Stripe says the platform must reverse or reconcile transfers itself ([Stripe Connect](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers)). Use `transfers.createReversal` for actual manager-funded amounts and `refunds.create` for the platform charge. The current helper has an explicit `reverseTransferAmount` and a separate `refundApplicationFee` option (`server/services/stripe-service.ts:815-982`). For this current separate-charge model there is no Stripe *application fee object* to refund; the Local Cooks fee is charge proceeds retained on the platform, so `refund_application_fee=false`/omitted and the platform-funded portion is part of the charge refund. If a historical source is a destination charge with a real application fee, detect its charge type and refund the corresponding application fee via Stripe's [application-fee refund API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/fee_refunds/create), without double funding; route unsupported legacy shapes to review.

Idempotency contract: one durable cancellation ID per booking and per-source refund operation IDs derived from booking/source/snapshot/cancellation version; unique indexes; booking and payment-source row locks; write cancellation time, tier and reserved amounts before provider calls; stable Stripe idempotency keys for reversal and refund; include operation ID in Stripe metadata; discover prior refund/reversal receipts before retry; reconcile webhooks against source totals and prevent a later transfer from paying out reserved refund money. If reversal lacks connected-account available balance, cancellation stays effective, refund is `processing/recovery_required`, and Local Cooks operations resolve funding under the same operation ID; no duplicate refund or false “refunded” email. Current recovery patterns at `server/services/booking-cancellation-refund.ts:185-228` and payout guard at `server/services/stripe-transfer-service.ts:189-223` are starting points, not proof that the new policy is implemented.

## 7. Open questions requiring owner or specialist decision

1. **Approval gate:** will counsel/acquirer approve the actual-fee deduction, its exact disclosure, and the exception hierarchy for mandatory/full remedies? If they reject it, the fallback is platform-funded full refunds in the early period; no silent estimate/cap substitute.
2. **Immediately penalized short-notice bookings:** permit a booking confirmed inside its first penalty window, impose a defined confirmation grace period, or block that checkout? If a grace exists, what exact elapsed time and minimum time-before-start apply?
3. **Guardrails:** approve the proposed 24-hour minimum full tier, 720-hour maximum window, five-tier limit, 1% increments, and whether/when Strict becomes selectable. Should daily/long storage have a separate policy product?
4. **Fee source:** can Stripe/account reporting isolate the exact original *card-processing* portion per charge, including any card-specific surcharge or monthly-billed adjustment? What is the fallback SLA when fee details are delayed? Who contacts the customer if classification cannot be completed?
5. **Tax:** accountant to document who supplies/collects/remits kitchen HST, whether the retained processing deduction and forfeited rental amount are taxable consideration under s. 182, credit-note treatment for each tier, and the Local Cooks registration number/rate shown on its fee receipt. A 15% NL example is not a universal rate.
6. **Manager funding:** if the connected account cannot fund a required reversal, will Local Cooks advance the refund and recover from later transfers, and under what manager agreement/reserve? This cannot delay the booking's cancellation.
7. **Terms and historical bookings:** who reviews uploaded attachments before publication, how are conflicting historical terms adjudicated, and which current bookings require fresh acceptance before capture? Preserve evidence of the exact file presented.
8. **Component scope:** does cancellation of kitchen time also cancel all unstarted equipment/storage by default, and what separate terms govern started storage, consumables, and committed preparation? Define per-component refund rules before enabling modular bundled checkout.
