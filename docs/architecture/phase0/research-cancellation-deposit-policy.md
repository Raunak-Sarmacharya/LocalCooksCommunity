# LocalCooks: cancellation, processing costs, and damage security

**Research/access date: 2026-10-11. Decision memo; no code or payment-account changes.**

This extends [research-booking-standards.md](research-booking-standards.md), especially sections 1–4, and resolves the policy choices in [phase0-evidence-pack.md](phase0-evidence-pack.md), especially H2, M1, M5, M9, and section 5. It does not repeat their architecture, notification, or timezone audit. LocalCooks' configuration is taken from the owner's brief; the sandbox observations are attributed to the evidence pack, not independently retested here. Proposed timings and policies below are **LocalCooks recommendations**, not claims of a universal industry standard. Legal enforceability, tax classification, and network eligibility remain professional/account-specific questions.

## Executive recommendation

1. **Keep authorization at request and capture on approval, with a 24 elapsed-hour decision deadline.** Before confirmation, withdrawal, decline, or expiry releases the entire authorization. After capture, cancellation and refunds follow the booking's accepted policy. Airbnb and Vrbo support a 24-hour request-to-book decision period; Stripe supports cancellation of an unfinished payment without a refund operation. [Airbnb request handling](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/28), [Vrbo booking requests](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-I-accept-a-booking-request), [Stripe cancellations/refunds](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds).
2. **Make cancellation self-serve and effective immediately, including late cancellation.** A manager may approve an *extra refund*, not veto cancellation or delay an already-owed policy refund. This follows Airbnb's guest flow and Turo's immediate cancellation model. [Airbnb guest cancellation](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/169), [Turo cancellation policy, revised April 14, 2026](https://turo.com/us/en/policies/cancellation).
3. **During pilots, refund the full amount paid for eligible early cancellations, including any actual service fee and its applicable tax; do not deduct Stripe costs from the chef.** LocalCooks absorbs the unrecovered processing cost on refunded funds. This is a recommended trust/acquisition expense, not a claim that Stripe returns its fee. Airbnb refunds guest service fees during free cancellation; Vrbo returns its processing fees on full refunds, unlike Stripe's standard pricing. [Airbnb service-fee refunds](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1345), [Vrbo processing/service-fee refunds](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/If-I-or-my-traveler-has-to-cancel-a-reservation-am-I-still-charged-a-booking-fee), [Stripe Canada pricing](https://stripe.com/ca/pricing).
4. **Launch with Flexible (24 hours) as default and a structured Moderate (72/24 hours) option.** Defer Strict and bespoke long-term/storage terms until reviewed. Show exact deadlines and dollars; freeze the accepted policy. Presets simplify comparison, while structured fields make refunds executable. The particular kitchen timings are proposals, informed by The Food Corridor's kitchen-specific cancellation guidance rather than copied lodging cutoffs. [Food Corridor pricing models](https://www.thefoodcorridor.com/toolkit-guide/pricing-models/).
5. **For damage security, use no captured deposit during the pilot: save the card with specific consent, record condition, and review claims.** Report incidents within 24 hours after use; give chefs 48 hours to respond; seek explicit payment approval for the assessed claim. Do not enable automatic damage debits merely because `setup_future_usage=off_session` is present. Stripe requires agreed purposes, timing, amount determination, and retained consent. [Stripe CIT/MIT rules](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/cits-and-mits).
6. **Disable Klarna for this B2B use case pending an expressly approved alternative.** Stripe's current Klarna compliance page lists business-to-business transactions as prohibited. Manual capture support does not cure that restriction. [Stripe Klarna compliance](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/klarna/compliance).

### Recommended refund matrix

This is the **proposed policy**, not current application behaviour. “100%” means the amount actually paid for that component, including its attributable tax where applicable. “Service fee” means LocalCooks' chef-facing fee, not Stripe's processing expense. When the configured service fee is zero, omit its label, line, percentage, explanatory copy, and refund copy from every customer surface; retain the zero internally for reconciliation.

Default Flexible cutoff: cancellation received **at or before start minus 24 elapsed hours** qualifies for the full refund. Optional Moderate: 100% at or before start minus 72 hours; 50% after that through start minus 24 hours; then 0%. This explicit inclusive boundary replaces ambiguous “before/inside the window” wording.

| Booking/payment state | Who cancels / cause | Kitchen refund | Equipment and session add-ons | HST / other tax charged | LocalCooks service fee, if charged | Stripe processing cost / chef deduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requested; authorized, uncaptured | Chef withdraws | Entire hold released | Entire hold released | Entire hold released | Entire hold released | No successful card charge; **no chef deduction** |
| Requested; authorized, uncaptured | Manager declines, no response by expiry, or platform cannot confirm | Entire hold released | Entire hold released | Entire hold released | Entire hold released | **No chef deduction** |
| Confirmed; paid; within full-refund period or eligible grace period | Chef cancels | 100% | 100% | 100% of tax actually charged | 100% | Platform absorbs unrecovered cost; **no chef deduction** |
| Confirmed; optional Moderate partial-refund period | Chef cancels | 50% | 50% for reserved equipment/session resources; exceptions below | Credit/recalculate tax on refunded components and retained cancellation amount; accountant rule required | 50% of fee attributable to the cancelled session | **No additional processing deduction**; platform absorbs cost on refunded portion |
| Confirmed; after final refundable cutoff, before use | Chef cancels | 0% by policy | 0% for reserved equipment/session resources; exceptions below | Reclassify/adjust as required for retained cancellation payment; do not assume all tax is refundable or retained unchanged | 0% by policy | No extra charge or duplicate fee; existing manager fee deduction continues on retained booking earnings |
| Confirmed; late cancellation | Chef cancels; manager grants voluntary relief | Chosen refund, at least policy entitlement | Chosen per-component relief; exceptions below | Corresponding accountant-approved credit/reclassification | Same refund proportion for related platform fee; 100% if entire unused booking is refunded | Platform absorbs unrecovered cost; **no chef deduction** |
| Confirmed; chef does not attend | Chef no-show, manager proves access was available | Same as late cancellation; no separate punitive no-show charge in pilot | Same as late cancellation; exceptions below | Accountant-approved cancellation/no-show tax treatment | Same as late cancellation | No extra processing deduction |
| Confirmed; cannot be supplied; no use | Manager cancels, manager fails to provide access, unsafe/unlicensed kitchen, or LocalCooks booking/payment error | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% | **Never deducted from chef**; platform covers pilot cost, with manager accountability separately |
| In use / partly supplied | Manager failure or unusable material component | Full refund if chef rejects before meaningful use; otherwise unused/failed supply plus reviewed remedy | Refund unavailable items; assess dependencies, including whether missing equipment defeats the entire booking | Accountant-approved credit for the remedy | Refund attributable fee; full fee if full booking refunded | No chef processing deduction |

**Add-on boundaries (recommendations):** ordinary unused cleaning and undelivered consumables are refunded, even if kitchen time is forfeited; exceptionally committed preparation/consumable costs require separately accepted, specific terms. Reserved equipment capacity follows the session policy. A one-off storage reservation that never begins follows the session policy; an already-running monthly storage agreement has its own termination/unused-period rules and is not silently cancelled with a cooking session. Damage security is never treated as booking revenue or a late-cancellation penalty. This component separation is consistent with Peerspace's cleaning exception and Turo's separate treatment of Extras/deposits. [Peerspace refund help](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119471-what-is-the-cancellation-and-refund-policy), [Turo cancellation policy](https://turo.com/us/en/policies/cancellation).

**Tax is not simply “refund 15%” or “keep 15%.”** NL's current HST rate is 15%, but retained cancellation/forfeiture amounts may have a different calculation from supplied kitchen rent. CRA describes tax-inclusive forfeited deposits, and Excise Tax Act section 182 addresses certain breach/termination payments to registrants. Obtain a written rule for each matrix row, including the platform fee's tax. [CRA current rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html), [CRA deposits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-special-cases.html), [Excise Tax Act s.182](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-15/section-182.html).

## Where the owner's model is right / where I would change it

**Keep:** no settled payment before manager approval; free pending withdrawal; a clearly marked late-cancellation boundary; manager discretion to refund *more* than policy requires; a separate damage/overstay process; saved-card preparation where properly consented. Peerspace likewise authorizes before approval and releases on decline; Vrbo explicitly permits custom refunds that meet or exceed the policy entitlement. [Peerspace request handling](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119524-what-happens-when-i-receive-a-booking-request), [Vrbo policy/custom refunds](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/What-are-the-cancellation-policy-options).

**Change:**

- Outside the penalty period, replace “request cancellation; manager approves” with “cancel booking; policy refund starts.” Delay creates uncertainty and can move a chef past a cutoff through no action of their own. Cancellation receipt time should determine entitlement.
- Inside the penalty period, cancel immediately, then offer “request an exception refund.” The manager sees the cutoff, policy amount, optional additional refund, and resulting payable; they do not decide whether the chef is allowed to stop attending.
- Replace “refund everything except Stripe's fee” with a genuine full refund in the pilot. Keeping a disclosed fee can be a commercial choice, but the surveyed policies do not establish a universal actual-processor-cost deduction. Avoid an admin appeal just to obtain what the site called a full refund.
- A captured damage deposit refunded after checkout is a predictable processing expense, even when every booking goes well. Do not add one simply because a manager can enter a deposit amount.
- Add explicit manager-failure remedies and policy precedence. Manager terms cannot remove platform-promised refunds; statutory rights override both where applicable.

**Evidence-pack consequence:** its sandbox booking paid $91.50, received $82.56 back, and lost $5.25 service fee plus $3.69 actual processing fee. Under the recommended full-refund row, that chef receives **$91.50**, not $82.56 or $87.81. The latter is the owner's proposed service-fee refund with processing still retained. These are calculations from the existing evidence, not a new Stripe test. [Evidence pack, walkthrough and H2/M5](phase0-evidence-pack.md).

## A. Cancellation approval: market practice and decision

**Recommendation: self-serve policy cancellation; human approval only for exceptions, amendments, and contested cause.** Manager-controlled request-to-book is useful before a binding rental is confirmed. It does not imply that ending a confirmed booking needs another approval. Product rationale: predictable cash outcome, prompt inventory release, fewer support cases, and a durable cutoff timestamp.

The useful comparison is **policy entitlement versus discretionary relief**, not simply whether a company uses the word “request”:

- **Airbnb:** guests cancel from Trips, see the refund beforehand, and Airbnb initiates the applicable refund as soon as cancellation occurs. Host-approved additional refunds are a separate route. [Guest cancellation](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/169), [refund components](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3066).
- **Vrbo / Expedia Group:** the current official host help on Vrbo's Australian surface says both refundable and nonrefundable bookings can be cancelled by the guest. A request status remains for cases that cannot self-serve, including bookings made before a policy existed; the host should process requests promptly, ideally within 24 hours. IPM/channel-manager handling is distinct. **Regional documentation caveat:** this is an official surface, not proof that every Canadian integration has identical buttons. It still does not support approval as a universal early-cancellation requirement. [Vrbo guest cancellations](https://www.vrbo.com/en-au/help/articles/Can-a-traveller-cancel-a-reservation).
- **Peerspace:** guests can withdraw pending requests without penalty and use platform cancellation for confirmed bookings. Its legal terms distinguish booking amendments from cancellation through the Cancel Booking function. A formal platform submission is a record requirement, not evidence of a host veto on an earned refund. [Guest cancellation help](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119168-can-i-cancel-my-booking), [Services Agreement, section 3.2(e)](https://www.peerspace.com/legal/terms).
- **Turo:** guests cancel through the site/app and cancellation is immediately effective. Refundable trips normally have free cancellation until 24 hours before start; modifications do not reset the original grace-period reference. These are trip rules, not a manager-approval model. [Cancellation policy](https://turo.com/us/en/policies/cancellation).
- **Getaround:** international/European help describes self-serve cancellation with an exact refund preview, a standard-car full-refund threshold more than 48 hours ahead, and an appeal after cancellation. Contested cases can pause the refund. This is not a current Canadian offering or a universal Stripe hold model. [International cancellation guide](https://fr.getaround.com/help/articles/77ae2d7092b0/intl).
- **Booking.com:** free cancellation has no cancellation fee; provider/rate policy determines late and nonrefundable outcomes. The official FAQ directs guests to their confirmation and cancellation process. Do not describe all provider-funded refunds as instant platform payouts or infer a universal processing-cost allocation. [Official guest FAQ](https://secure.booking.com/faq.en-gb.html?aid=330843), [provider policies in guest terms](https://m.booking.com/content/terms.en-gb.html).
- **OpenTable / Resy:** restaurant card guarantees, booking deposits and prepayments are different products. OpenTable's current diner help says eligible deposit cancellation triggers an automatic refund; restaurant-issued cancellation-fee refunds remain restaurant-controlled. Resy's prepaid-reservation help likewise describes automatic refunds before cutoff and restaurant discretion after cutoff. These reservation deposits are not damage security. [OpenTable policies](https://help.opentable.com/s/article/What-are-cancellation-no-show-and-deposit-policies?language=en_US), [Resy prepaid cancellation](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-cancel-and-refund-a-prepaid-reservation-SJRfzDXIu).
- **ClassPass:** normally self-serve without penalty until 12 hours ahead; late cancellation/no-show produces the disclosed fee or retained credits. Its capacity-protection rationale is relevant; the membership/credits model is different. [Cancellation policy](https://help.classpass.com/hc/en-us/articles/207942743-What-is-the-reservation-cancellation-policy).
- **Uber / Lyft:** riders cancel directly; qualifying fees compensate driver time/effort and can be challenged. They are not described as unrecovered processor fees. Cutoffs depend on product/location, so copying a universal two-minute rule would be wrong. [Uber cancellation fees](https://help.uber.com/en/riders/article/node-title?nodeId=5f6415dc-dfdb-4d64-927a-66bb06bc4f82), [Lyft Canada cancellation/no-show help](https://help.lyft.com/hc/en-ca/rider/articles/115012922687-Cancellation-policy-for-passengers).

### Kitchen-specific evidence: useful but not a single standard

- **The Kitchen Door / The Food Corridor:** Kitchen Door's published terms put the kitchen hire agreement between business client and kitchen host and require a listing cancellation policy. Food Corridor's software supports kitchen-specific penalty windows and flat/percentage charges; its hourly billing occurs after use. These are good comparators for structured B2B rules, not proof of a Stripe collect/refund standard. [Kitchen Door terms, 1.3 and 2.6.5](https://www.thekitchendoor.com/tos), [Food Corridor cancellation/billing](https://help.thefoodcorridor.com/en/articles/1413981-how-are-food-businesses-charged).
- **One World KitchenShare (Indiana):** contract/deposit onboarding and contact-based cancellations coexist with reservations usually approved within 24 hours. Its FAQ does not establish deposit refund timing or processor-cost treatment. It is one operator, not every similarly named ShareKitchen business. [Operator FAQ](https://www.kitchenshare.net/faq).
- **Share A Kitchen (Illinois):** publicly advertises no security deposit, illustrating that deposits are not inevitable. Its published page does not resolve cancellation/refund economics. [Getting started](https://www.shareakitchen.com/start.html).
- **Kitchen United / PREP:** Kitchen United's public site currently describes restaurant takeout/delivery; PREP's official site provides membership/location contacts. No sufficiently documented public booking-level cancellation, deposit-release, or actual-processing-fee policy was verified for these businesses. Do not infer it from a sales funnel. [Kitchen United](https://kitchenunited.com/), [PREP contacts](https://prepkitchens.com/contact/).
- **CommiKitchen / “ShareKitchen”:** searches did not establish an unambiguous current primary-source policy for the requested names. No policy is attributed to them. Obtain the exact company URL or signed operator agreement before treating one as a benchmark.

**Decision:** B2B kitchen contracts justify eligibility review and possibly longer cancellation notice for exclusive production blocks. They do not justify making an advertised refundable booking subject to an unanswered manager message.

## B. Processing costs: what is standard, who pays, and Canadian limits

### Do the top companies pass through the actual card-processing loss?

**No universal rule was verified. Do not claim to know competitors' processor contracts or internal accounting.** The public evidence establishes customer outcomes:

- **Airbnb:** current home-stay help refunds its guest service fee within free cancellation and when a host elects a full refund afterward. It does not disclose a separate deduction of the actual processor cost from those refunds. **Inference:** Airbnb's full-refund promise leaves its own processing economics to Airbnb/its commercial arrangements; the source does not prove whether a payment supplier reimburses it. [Service-fee refunds](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1345).
- **Vrbo:** on its documented payment service, a 100% refund returns guest service fee and host commission/payment-processing fees; partial refunds reverse processing cost on refunded funds, while booking/service fees are not returned. IPMs must check their own merchant processor. Vrbo is therefore a counterexample to “the chef must pay because every platform does”; it is not evidence that Stripe does the same. [Vrbo full/partial fee refunds](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/If-I-or-my-traveler-has-to-cancel-a-reservation-am-I-still-charged-a-booking-fee).
- **Peerspace:** its June 2025 fee article defines the guest “processing fee” as a broader platform operating/support fee, not an itemized Stripe invoice. It says refundable during the qualified grace period and nonrefundable afterward. Its June 2026 cancellation help simultaneously lists 100% tiers and says customary fees are retained. **Unresolved official-source inconsistency:** the exact all-fees outcome outside grace cannot safely be generalized from those pages. A disclosed platform fee is distinguishable from LocalCooks deducting an unpredictable actual card cost. [Guest-fee definition/treatment](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119157-what-is-the-peerspace-processing-fee-for-guests), [June 2026 refund help](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119471-what-is-the-cancellation-and-refund-policy).
- **Turo:** publishes full refunds for eligible free cancellations and separately describes late cancellation components. No actual-Stripe-fee haircut is specified in that policy; this does not reveal its internal processor economics. [Cancellation policy](https://turo.com/us/en/policies/cancellation).

### Stripe Canada: cost versus refund entitlement

Standard Canadian online pricing currently lists **2.9% + CA$0.30 for domestic cards**, plus 0.8% for international cards and 2% where currency conversion is required. Standard card refunds have no additional refund-issuance fee, but original processing, Connect and conversion fees are not returned. Custom pricing may differ. Manual capture is not “manual card entry,” which has a separate surcharge. [Canada pricing and refund FAQ](https://stripe.com/ca/pricing).

Cancelling an unfinished payment is free under Stripe's refund documentation. For LocalCooks' **uncaptured card** PaymentIntent, release the authorization rather than capture and refund. Do not generalize this into “every authorization-related product is free”: custom/network/other Stripe-product fees require account confirmation. Hold release and bank-visible availability are different events. [Stripe cancel/refund distinction](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds), [manual card authorization](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).

**Recommendation: accept the cost during pilots, measure it, then review the manager/platform split.** Record cancellation rate, original fee, refund amount, and recovery failure. An expected pilot budget can be estimated as eligible-refund rate × average unrecovered fee, plus separately assessed Connect costs. Do not announce a “free cancellation” policy and quietly subtract a business expense.

**Separate charges/transfers consequence:** the charge is on the platform; its refund does not automatically reverse the manager transfer. Recover the relevant transfer or adjust the payable separately, and provide liquidity when recovery is delayed/insufficient. With the evidence pack's $91.50 charge, $3.69 processing fee and $82.56 transfer, full transfer recovery plus the platform's retained $5.25 leaves an unrecovered **$3.69** when the customer receives $91.50. Do not recover the same original processing fee from both chef and manager. [Stripe separate transfers/refunds](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers?locale=es-ES), [sandbox amounts](phase0-evidence-pack.md).

For partial refunds, define the resulting manager payable explicitly: retained manager supply/cancellation entitlement minus the processing cost allocated to retained earnings; the platform bears the portion allocated to refunded money under this proposal. Refund tax and fee lines according to their own agreed/accountant-approved rules. A percentage reversal of the transfer is not by itself proof that the commercial allocation is correct.

### Is retaining the fee legally acceptable in Canada / NL?

**Answer: potentially as a clearly agreed contractual cancellation cost in a genuine B2B transaction, but no blanket legal clearance; not where an overriding refund right requires otherwise.** That is a legal-risk assessment, not a legal opinion or a claim that Canadian law automatically requires every voluntary refund to include processing costs.

The Competition Act's misleading-pricing provision addresses unattainable advertised prices caused by fixed obligatory fees, with an exception for charges imposed on the purchaser by legislation. Stripe is a merchant business expense, not such a government charge. The provision is not limited in its text to household consumers. A contingent cancellation deduction is not automatically identical to an unavoidable checkout charge, but misleading “100% refund/free cancellation” representations remain a concern. Show any mandatory booking fee in the attainable price and any contingent deduction prominently before agreement. [Competition Act s.74.01(1), (1.1)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-34/section-74.01.html), [Competition Bureau amendment guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/education-and-outreach/guide-june-2024-amendments-competition-act).

NL's Act defines a consumer by personal/family/household purpose and excludes business-purpose activity: being a small sole proprietor does not itself make a chef a consumer. For covered consumers, misleading omissions and unconscionable practices are prohibited; distance-sale disclosure/acceptance/copy requirements apply. **Section 33 requires refund without deduction within 15 days for cancellation under section 32**; this is not a general convenience-cancellation right. Section 3 prevents contractual waiver of protected rights. [NL Consumer Protection and Business Practices Act, ss.2–3, 7–9, 28–33](https://www.assembly.nl.ca/legislation/sr/statutes/c31-1.htm).

**If the owner nevertheless retains processing cost (fallback, not recommendation):** get counsel's approval; say “refund minus the original nonrefundable processing cost,” never “100%” or “free”; show the calculation/cap before booking and the exact CAD deduction once capture establishes it; include international-card variation; distinguish it from LocalCooks' service fee; waive it for manager/platform failure and any mandatory remedy. An uncapped “actual Stripe fee” known only later is less predictable than an expressly accepted capped cancellation charge. Do not add a margin or a second refund fee under the guise of Stripe cost. Review card-surcharge rules if the design is actually a card-payment surcharge. Discretionary refunds of that deduction should have published reasons and a support deadline, not an unexplained favour.

## C. Presets, structured fields, and immutable terms

**Recommendation: two named presets backed by explicit fields; an uploaded agreement supplements them.** A single numeric manager window can work for a pilot only if the platform also fixes refund percentages, affected components, fee/tax treatment, cutoff inclusivity, grace period, no-show rules, and exceptions. “24 hours” alone is not a cancellation policy.

Proposed launch options:

- **Flexible:** 100% through 24 elapsed hours before start; 0% thereafter, subject to component exceptions and mandatory remedies.
- **Moderate:** 100% through 72 hours; 50% from after 72 hours through 24 hours; 0% thereafter. Validate against manager utilization before enabling.
- **Strict, later/admin-reviewed:** candidate 100% through seven days, 50% through 72 hours, 0% afterward for long exclusive blocks. Do not launch it as an unrestricted manager text box or assume Airbnb's Strict has this meaning.
- **Proposed grace period for either launch preset:** cancellation within 24 hours of paid confirmation **and** at least 48 hours before start receives the full refund. No rolling reset through amendments. This is a product choice: Peerspace uses a 24-hour/48-hour combination, whereas Airbnb short stays require confirmation at least seven days before check-in. [Peerspace grace help](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119168-can-i-cancel-my-booking), [Airbnb current policy options](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/475).

Presets are common but labels are not interchangeable: Airbnb currently has Flexible, Moderate, Limited and Firm, with Strict restricted to invited hosts; Vrbo offers its own date tiers and permits only custom refunds at least equal to policy entitlement. [Airbnb options](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/475), [Vrbo options](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/What-are-the-cancellation-policy-options).

**Required policy fields (product recommendation):** policy ID/version; elapsed-hour thresholds and refund percentages; session/series scope; exact start and kitchen timezone; grace reference; component-specific exceptions; platform-fee rule; processing-cost bearer; no-show/access definition; manager-failure remedy; exceptional-circumstance route; deposit/claim method and deadlines. Record add-on preparation commitments separately. For nonconsecutive sessions, evaluate each unstarted session using its own frozen deadline; a monthly storage termination is separate.

**Required booking snapshot (product recommendation):** freeze the complete substantive policy, quote/line amounts/tax bases, fee rate/amount, legal supplier, local display deadlines and corresponding instants, upload content/hash/version, affirmative acceptance record, and card mandate. Freeze at request so manager approval cannot silently substitute harsher terms. Preserve original and accepted amendment versions; renew consent for material changes; do not retroactively alter earned rights. Vrbo expressly preserves the booked policy when seasonal rules change, and its displayed cancellation policy overrides a conflicting rental agreement. These are useful precedence examples, not proof of its internal storage design. [Vrbo policy/versioning](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/What-are-the-cancellation-policy-options).

An uploaded PDF alone cannot generate a reliable refund preview or guarantee consistent app/email language. Put the governing economics next to the booking action, with the full retainable agreement linked. Recommend precedence: mandatory law → LocalCooks manager-failure/exception protections → frozen structured booking policy → consistent supplemental kitchen rules. Review that hierarchy with counsel.

## D. Manager cancellations and manager no-shows

**Recommendation: full customer refund for an unprovided booking, immediate release of any security hold, no manager payout, and practical rebooking assistance.** Resolve the chef's remedy independently of whether a manager's penalty is excused. A manager cannot avoid responsibility by asking the chef to select “I changed my plans.” Do not promise reimbursement of lost food, wages, travel, or lost business unless funded and contractually defined.

Official benchmarks: Airbnb imposes host-cancellation fees and can block calendars or suspend hosts; it forbids encouraging guests to cancel for the host. Turo gives full refunds/rebooking help on host failure and treats a host absent for 30 minutes as a no-show. Its Canada cancellation/no-show penalties are documented separately. Vrbo can charge cancellation fees, suspend listings and remove Premier Host status. Peerspace's refund help contemplates full guest refunds and host penalties. [Airbnb host policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/990), [Turo host/no-show policy](https://turo.com/us/en/policies/cancellation), [Turo guest remedy](https://help.turo.com/en_us/host-no-shows-HyejENeNc), [Vrbo host policy overview](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/Partner-Cancellation-Fee-Policy), [Peerspace refund help](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119471-what-is-the-cancellation-and-refund-policy).

**Pilot operations:**

- At start, chef contacts the manager through the recorded booking channel and reports inaccessible premises to LocalCooks. Proposed grace: **15 minutes** for access resolution for hourly rentals; do not require waiting in unsafe premises. If access remains unavailable and the chef declines to proceed, refund the unused booking in full. Where a late start is accepted, agree the extension or missing-time refund explicitly.
- If a material equipment/storage failure defeats the intended production session, review the whole booking remedy, not just the small add-on price.
- First avoidable cancellation: documented coaching and reason; repeated incidents: review/pause the listing. Track no-access reports separately from ordinary chef cancellation. Genuine regulatory closure/safety repair may waive a penalty while preserving the chef's refund.
- LocalCooks covers pilot refund processing costs. Later, a reviewed manager agreement may allocate actual avoidable-cancellation costs or a proportionate penalty to the manager; never deduct them from the chef's remedy.
- Offer another suitable licensed kitchen where feasible, with chef acceptance of revised dates/location/equipment and price. A full refund remains available; do not substitute expiring platform credit without consent.

## E. Damage deposits: economics, alternatives, and the startup choice

### Four approaches and what each solves

**1. Collect and refund a captured deposit:** strongest immediate cash backing, but creates cash/refund handling and a fee loss when refunded under Stripe standard pricing. A separately charged **CA$200** domestic-card deposit costs **CA$6.10** at 2.9% + $0.30, even when all $200 is returned. Adding $200 to an existing card charge adds approximately $5.80 in percentage cost rather than another fixed $0.30; actual pricing can vary. These are illustrative calculations, excluding other product costs. If the platform promises a fully refundable deposit, fund the expense or contractually allocate it to the manager; returning $193.90 is not a full $200 refund. [Stripe Canada pricing/refunds](https://stripe.com/ca/pricing).

Vrbo explicitly offers upfront refundable damage deposits as well as card-on-file and protection options; its guest help describes a 14-day assessment/refund period. This shows collect/refund exists at scale, not that the operator loses no processing fees under LocalCooks' contract. [Vrbo damage deposits](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-damage-deposits-work).

**2. A separate uncaptured authorization near use:** avoids routine capture/refund expense and gives temporary payment backing, subject to issuer and network eligibility. Keep it separate from the rental PaymentIntent, already captured at confirmation. Stripe's online windows are Visa CIT seven days and Visa MIT **four days 18 hours**; Mastercard, Amex and Discover commonly seven days. Use the actual `capture_before`, not a seven-day assumption or Canadian lodging analogy. [Stripe card holds/windows](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).

**Extended holds are conditional, not a default kitchen capability.** Stripe documents no regional restriction, but card/MCC/transaction eligibility and pricing access matter: IC+ access or contact Stripe on blended pricing; Mastercard all categories up to 30 days; Amex lodging/vehicle rental only; Discover selected categories. Visa's other-category extension is CIT-only with an additional **0.08%** and approximately 29 days 18 hours. A scheduled saved-card MIT must not be assumed eligible for Visa's other-category extension. [Stripe extended authorization](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/extended-authorization?platform=web&ui=stripe-hosted).

**Incremental authorization is not renewal.** It increases an eligible *uncaptured* authorization, requires supported network/account access and estimated-amount consent, and does not extend the original authorization expiry. It cannot increase the already-captured rental payment to obtain a new damage hold. Do not repeatedly roll a hold merely to avoid its expiry. [Stripe incremental authorization and expiry](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/incremental-authorization?platform=web&ui=stripe-hosted), [increment API eligibility](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment_intents/increment_authorization).

**3. No deposit; saved card; evidence-backed claim:** less checkout friction and no successful-deposit refund cost, but no guaranteed funds, possible authentication/decline/dispute, and human review expense. Saving a card only prepares the payment credential; it does not establish liability or unlimited payment permission. [Stripe SetupIntents/permission and recovery](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/setup-intents).

Useful precedents, with important qualifications:

- Airbnb generally prohibits host security deposits, with specified exceptions, and routes damage reimbursement through its Resolution Center. Hosts should file within 14 days; guests have 24 hours to respond; further review/appeal precedes collection. Host Damage Protection is expressly not insurance. LocalCooks has no equivalent funded guarantee merely by copying this workflow. [Airbnb deposits](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/140), [Host Damage Protection](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/279), [guest damage process](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1415).
- Turo requires initial damage reporting within 24 hours and condition evidence. It also collects deposits for some Canadian trips, so “Turo never takes deposits” is incorrect; its Canada help describes automatic release/refund initiation 80 hours after trip end when requirements are met. [Damage reporting](https://help.turo.com/reporting-damage-Bk7aIWhHi), [Canada deposits](https://help.turo.com/en_us/-ryCqIcvAT).
- Peerspace requires reporting non-emergency issues within 72 hours. It also permits disclosed refundable day-of deposits collected outside its app, says most hosts do not collect one, and prohibits duplicate recovery through its guarantee. [Incident reporting](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119485-what-do-i-do-if-there-is-an-issue-resulting-from-a-booking), [deposit rules](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119427-can-i-require-guests-to-pay-a-security-deposit).
- Vrbo's card-on-file option has no preauthorization hold and a 14-day host claim window. It is therefore another actual no-upfront-collection precedent alongside its captured-deposit product. [Guest deposit options](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-damage-deposits-work).

**4. Third-party damage protection / insurance:** can fund covered losses beyond a small hold, but coverage, exclusions, deductibles, underwriting and claims administration must match commercial food production and equipment use. Vrbo's protection is an optional insurance product; Airbnb's damage guarantee has a different legal character. Do not sell “insurance” or promise “covered damage” until a broker/carrier approves the actual Canadian commercial-kitchen product and LocalCooks' distribution role. [Vrbo protection description](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-damage-deposits-work), [Airbnb protection limitations](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/279).

### Card-network limit: a hold is not permission to seize disputed damage money

Visa's **18 April 2026** public rules, section 5.8.8.3 / Table 5-19, printed pp.451–455, restrict delayed charges to eligible merchant types, including MCC 7394 equipment rental. They distinguish damages from other extras; require actual repair/replacement-cost support; prohibit using an advance payment for damage/theft/loss of use; and set documentation and dispute/waiting conditions. The documented unilateral damage route includes a **20-business-day wait**, an alternative-estimate opportunity and a 90-day processing boundary. Do not assume a commercial-kitchen marketplace qualifies or that a two-day claim clock overrides it. [Visa current public rules](https://usa.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/download/about-visa/visa-rules-public.pdf).

Mastercard's publicly served **10 June 2025** processing rules require consent to estimated authorization amounts and separate loss/theft/damage transactions in their lodging/cruise/vehicle-rental provisions. Those specialized provisions are not automatic kitchen eligibility. Obtain Stripe/acquirer confirmation of current rules and MCC treatment across accepted brands. [Mastercard rules, section 3.3.1, printed p.102](https://www.mastercard.us/content/dam/public/mastercardcom/na/global-site/documents/transaction-processing-rules.pdf).

**Consequence (inference):** a short near-use card hold cannot support a promise to collect every disputed damage claim before it expires. Network-compliant claim adjudication and collection are distinct from booking/checkout approval. Generic Stripe API capability must not be treated as approval of a damage-deposit or delayed-charge business model.

### Recommended pilot: no captured deposit; specific saved-card agreement; voluntary claim payment first

All timings below are proposed LocalCooks operating standards, not card-network permission:

1. **At booking:** show permitted damage/extra-cleaning/overstay categories, evidence requirements, rates, payment process and liability boundaries; obtain affirmative agreement. Show no “refundable deposit” if no funds or hold will be collected. Cardholder authority and chef/business authority must both be established where different people are involved.
2. **24 hours before start:** send an operational reminder with agreed cleanup/overstay rates and condition checklist; no automatic damage hold in the pilot. If the mandate/card is missing or invalid, ask for an authenticated update. Do not silently cancel a paid booking for a new deposit requirement.
3. **Start/end:** record condition and actual access/exit times with both parties able to submit evidence. A checkout declaration is not admission to later damage, and normal wear/pre-existing defects are excluded from recovery.
4. **Within 24 hours after actual checkout, or scheduled end if no checkout is recorded:** manager reports the incident with initial photos, explanation, and proposed amount. Inspect before the next user where possible. Late evidence is exceptional/admin-reviewed, not a new unlimited debit right.
5. **Within one business day of report:** LocalCooks screens the claim for causation, prior condition, proportionality, receipts/estimates and insurance/other recoveries. Notify the chef of evidence and amount; give **48 elapsed hours** to reply, extendable where notice delivery or complexity warrants.
6. **Target resolution within seven calendar days of report:** obtain explicit agreement and an authenticated payment for the assessed amount. If disputed or unanswered, pause collection and adjudicate; send an invoice or use the legally reviewed recovery route. **Silence is not fresh charge consent.** Do not capture a generic deposit or charge damage merely to beat an expiry. Network waiting periods may make collection substantially later than this resolution target.
7. **Off-session collection:** allow only for the specifically agreed, eligible purpose and amount under an approved mandate/network process. For pilot damage claims, require fresh approval of the assessed amount and prefer on-session payment. A routine, objectively evidenced overstay may use a separately accepted rate-based mandate after notice/review, subject to network confirmation. Authentication-required payments return the chef to an on-session flow; unsuccessful collection does not guarantee manager reimbursement. [Stripe saved-card agreement requirements](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/cits-and-mits), [authentication recovery](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/setup-intents).
8. **Appeal:** proposed 30 calendar days after a LocalCooks decision/charge for internal review, without limiting statutory or issuer dispute rights. Do not double-collect from a chef, insurer, deposit or manager guarantee. Do not delay an unrelated cancellation refund during a claim.

This approach accepts collection risk deliberately and avoids promising AirCover-scale protection. Obtain ordinary chef/manager commercial insurance appropriate to shared-kitchen operations; a card is not insurance. If managers require stronger security now, scope an individually reviewed arrangement rather than launch an unsupported universal deposit.

### Conditional later option: short separate hold for eligible bookings

If Stripe/acquirer explicitly permits this security use for LocalCooks' actual MCC/brands, pilot a separate hold **24 hours before start**, for sessions **no longer than 24 hours** and an agreed amount (for example CA$200, subject to manager-risk validation). Require the returned expiry to cover checkout plus 24-hour reporting, 48-hour response and at least a six-hour operational margin; otherwise place the approved hold nearer start or use the no-hold claim flow. Do not assume an off-session Visa hold can cover all those intervals.

Release immediately when the manager approves a clean checkout; auto-release by **checkout +24 hours** if no incident is filed. If a claim is filed, release the undisputed remainder where supported and collect only an approved amount by a permitted process. If adjudication cannot finish lawfully before expiry, release the hold and pursue the agreed claim/payment route. Do not capture the whole hold just to retain it for a dispute. Longer sessions, recurring storage and late-discovered damage need another approved arrangement. Partial capture generally releases the balance and usually permits only one capture. [Stripe hold/capture mechanics](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).

**This is a contingent option, not the launch recommendation.** The 20-business-day Visa delayed-damage route is incompatible with a standard short hold; fresh voluntary payment or another expressly permitted collection mechanism is required. [Visa rules, Table 5-19](https://usa.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/download/about-visa/visa-rules-public.pdf).

## F. Manager response, reminders, and far-advance bookings

**Recommendation: keep 24 elapsed hours, backed by automated expiry, visible countdown, reliable notifications, and a minimum booking lead time.** This is market-standard for Airbnb and Vrbo request-to-book, not every marketplace: Peerspace's June 2026 help says **two business days**. [Airbnb response rules](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/28), [Vrbo requests](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-I-accept-a-booking-request), [Peerspace current response period](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119524-what-happens-when-i-receive-a-booking-request).

Verified notification practices: Vrbo sends new-request emails, supports immediate SMS alerts, and can suspend listings after repeated expired requests. Its own help pages differ on the precise suspension threshold, so do not copy one as a universal rule. Airbnb encourages immediate request notifications and measures prompt response; Peerspace sends email/text with payout details. **No reliable public primary source was verified for exact Airbnb/Vrbo “12-hour and 22-hour” request reminders.** Those must be labelled LocalCooks choices. [Vrbo immediate SMS](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-I-receive-text-alerts-SMS-when-a-traveler-submits-an-inquiry), [Vrbo request escalation](https://help.vrbo.com/articles/How-do-I-accept-a-booking-request), [Vrbo alternate threshold page](https://help.vrbo.com/category/undefined/articles/Why-is-my-listing-hidden), [Airbnb notification setup](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/resources/hosting-homes/a/how-to-pick-your-booking-settings-619), [Peerspace notifications](https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119524-what-happens-when-i-receive-a-booking-request).

Use the prior memo's proposed cadence: immediate manager/chef acknowledgement; manager reminder at 12 hours; final reminder two hours before expiry; admin escalation for failed delivery or unresolved urgent requests; at expiry release the hold and notify both. Reminders never extend the deadline or auto-approve a request. For a full 24-hour decision allowance plus preparation, propose a 48-hour minimum request lead time in the pilot; shorter lead time needs a deliberately shortened, clearly displayed decision deadline or a staffed instant-book path.

**Far-advance booking does not require holding the rental authorization until use in the owner's current flow.** Capture on manager approval within 24 hours; the booking can begin weeks later because the rental is already paid. Expiry matters if approval/capture is delayed or if collection is deliberately postponed. Damage security would need its own near-use authorization. [Stripe authorization/capture expiry](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).

If capture fails/expires before confirmation, require a fresh authenticated payment authorization and keep the booking in payment-recovery status with a short disclosed deadline. Do not label it confirmed or recapture an expired authorization. If a future product defers payment until near use, save with specific scheduled-payment consent, make a new authorization/charge at the disclosed time, allow an authentication/payment update period, and define failure/cancellation outcomes. Repeated automatic reauthorizations can create overlapping holds and are not an indefinite extension strategy. [Stripe SetupIntents/future payments](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/setup-intents), [card authorization expiry](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).

Immediate manager transfer also creates recovery/liquidity risk long before cooking. The prior memo recommends delayed settlement; this memo does not assume that change has happened. Decide the payment promise and reserve policy separately from the cancellation entitlement. [Separate-transfer recovery](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers?locale=es-ES), [prior settlement recommendation](research-booking-standards.md).

## G. Klarna / BNPL risk note

**Disable Klarna for commercial kitchen rentals in the pilot.** Stripe's current Klarna rules expressly prohibit B2B. Klarna's separate direct documentation describes B2B through a third-party flow; that does not establish availability or permission through LocalCooks' Canadian Stripe integration. [Stripe restriction](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/klarna/compliance), [Klarna separate B2B flow](https://docs.klarna.com/acquirer/klarna/web-payments/additional-resources/use-cases/b2b-purchases/).

Manual capture itself is supported: Stripe's Klarna guide specifies a 28-day authorization deadline. Therefore “Klarna cannot do manual capture” is the wrong reason to disable it. Separate merchant eligibility, service/fulfillment, refund, Connect and saved-payment rules still matter. Setting `setup_future_usage` also changes the available Klarna payment options; a Klarna purchase must not be assumed to save a general-purpose card usable for damage holds. [Stripe Klarna capture and setup behaviour](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/klarna/accept-a-payment?payment-ui=direct-api&web-or-mobile=web).

## Suggested disclosures and consent copy

These are original drafting suggestions for the recommended policy; obtain legal/accounting/payment-provider approval before publishing. Replace brackets with actual frozen dates, amounts, rates, support identity and timezones. Generate fee language conditionally; when service fee = 0, the customer sees no fee line or fee explanation.

**Before request / payment authorization**

> Your booking request will be sent to the kitchen manager. We’ll place a temporary hold of [CA$ total] on your card and charge it only if your request is approved by [date, time, kitchen timezone]. You can withdraw before confirmation at no cost. If your request is declined or expires, we’ll release the hold. Your bank decides when the released amount becomes available.

**Flexible cancellation summary, beside the booking action**

> Cancel by [date, time, kitchen timezone] for a full refund of the amount you paid. After that deadline, kitchen time and reserved equipment are nonrefundable under this policy. [Show any separately governed add-on/storage terms.] You can cancel in your booking details at any time. If you need an exception, you can ask the kitchen manager or contact LocalCooks.

**Refund preview**

> If you cancel now, your refund will be [CA$ amount]. [Itemized kitchen, add-ons and tax; include service fee only if charged.] Your booking will be cancelled immediately, and we’ll start the eligible refund to your original payment method. Your bank determines when it appears in your account.

**Late cancellation / manager review**

> This cancellation is after the deadline for a policy refund. Your booking will be cancelled now. You may request an additional refund, and the kitchen manager will review it. You’ll receive a written decision and any refund details.

Manager-facing equivalent:

> The chef cancelled after [deadline]. The policy refund is [CA$ amount]. You may offer an additional refund of [amount]. This will reduce your booking earnings by [amount]; LocalCooks will show the corresponding tax and fee adjustment before you confirm.

**Manager failure**

> If the kitchen manager cancels or cannot provide the booked kitchen, we’ll refund the amount paid for the unprovided booking. Contact LocalCooks through your booking details if you cannot access the kitchen. Where possible, we’ll help you find another suitable kitchen.

**Pilot saved-card agreement: affirmative checkbox, not a generic future-purchases clause**

> I authorize LocalCooks to save this payment method for this booking and the payment purposes described in the Damage and Additional Charges Policy [version/link]. Possible charges are documented damage I am responsible for, cleaning beyond the agreed checkout standard, and recorded extra use at [CA$ rate / billing increment / maximum authorized amount]. LocalCooks will send the evidence, amount and proposed payment date before collection. I have [48 hours] to respond to the claim notice. For damage claims during the pilot, I will be asked to approve and pay the assessed amount. Any charge without my participation must be permitted by the agreed policy and applicable payment rules. A booking cancellation does not authorize a new damage charge.

The full agreement should identify the legal charging entity/beneficiary, whether later payments are one-time/unscheduled or recurring, claim reporting cutoff, exact calculation/cap, excluded wear/pre-existing damage, disputed-claim process, notice channel, receipt/descriptor, authentication fallback, and how to revoke future-payment consent/contact support without misrepresenting accrued liabilities. If no authority exists for a category, omit it. A technical setting cannot substitute for this agreement or network approval. [Stripe mandate requirements](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/setup-intents), [Stripe service terms, 4.7](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa-services-terms).

**Conditional separate hold, only if approved and enabled**

> This booking requires a separate security hold of [CA$ amount]. We’ll place it [24 hours before start / exact timestamp]. It reduces your available card balance while active. We’ll release it when checkout is approved, or by [checkout +24 hours] if no incident is reported. A reported issue will follow our evidence and review process; we won’t charge the full hold simply because a claim is open. Claims that require longer review may be handled by a separate agreed payment. Your bank controls the release display timing.

**If the owner selects processing-cost retention despite the recommendation**

> After confirmation, an eligible cancellation refund excludes the original payment-processing cost, up to [CA$ disclosed cap]. This cost varies by card and will be shown in your payment receipt and cancellation preview. Your refund today would be [CA$ total minus exact cost]. This deduction does not apply if the manager or LocalCooks cannot provide your booking, or where a full refund is required by law.

Do not publish that fallback beside “full refund,” “100% refunded,” or “free cancellation.” Do not imply it is a fee Stripe charges the chef for requesting a refund.
