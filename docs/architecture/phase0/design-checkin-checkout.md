# Kitchen visit check-in and check-out: research, audit, and target design

**Status:** proposal for owner review, 2026-10-12. **Scope:** hourly kitchen bookings, including nonconsecutive and overnight visits. Storage has a separate physical custody lifecycle and must not be auto-emptied by this design. This was a read-only code and public-source review; the booking 117 observations below are the supplied live findings, not a new environment test. No database, Stripe, Vercel, or Inngest operation was performed. The owner decisions in `PHASE0_LOG.md` (especially D8 and D11) and Phase 5 in `booking-target-phases.md` govern implementation.

## 1. Product decision in one page

The reservation gives a person the right to use a kitchen for **scheduled instants**. Reaching the start moves a confirmed visit into its booked use window; the right to use ends at the scheduled end, and the booking automatically moves to **Ended** no later than that end plus a short operational grace. A tap may record arrival, departure, condition, or a problem, but silence cannot hold a booking open, prove absence, impose an overstay fee, or block normal closeout. An actual access failure, safety issue, disputed attendance, claim, refund, or transfer is its own case with its own deadline and owner. For multiple visits, the booking's service ends after the **last** scheduled visit ends plus grace, while each visit has its own condition record and inspection task.

Recommended initial defaults for owner approval: show the day-of card at local midnight; open an optional arrival action 15 minutes before start; show a departure reminder 15 minutes before end; end the booked use right at scheduled end and automatically mark the booking **Ended at scheduled end + 15-minute operational grace** (the grace is not permission to occupy extra time); request manager inspection at departure or scheduled end; auto-close an unanswered ordinary inspection **24 hours after the later of scheduled end and departure report**, capped by a separately approved maximum from scheduled end. Keep a customer access/remedy deadline and a timely submitted claim separate from that auto-close. These numbers are proposals, not current policy. If a manager reports that the kitchen is still occupied, the booking still reaches Ended; the incident and next-booking protection remain active. Never infer an extra charge from a timer or a missing tap.

## 4. Target domain model and state transitions

**One kitchen visit source:** `kitchen_booking_visits` becomes the sole mutable record for each booked contiguous block, including a one-block booking. Today `ensureKitchenBookingVisits` explicitly returns no rows for fewer than two blocks (`server/services/kitchen-booking-visits.ts:7-36`), so this is a real migration, not a reader rename. `kitchen_bookings` retains commitment/payment state, policy snapshot, and derived aggregate read fields only. New writers never mutate booking-level `checkin*` / `checkout*` columns. Historical readers can display a tagged legacy projection until all affected obligations are resolved; do not erase evidence or invent historical attendance. Every confirmed booking must have at least one visit created in the same transaction as confirmation or via idempotent migration before target readers are enabled. Separate storage check-in/out remains separate.

Store each visit's `scheduled_start_at` and `scheduled_end_at` as UTC instants plus IANA location timezone, original local date/time and chosen offset (DST fold), schedule revision, and immutable duties/instruction version accepted for that booking. Derive a displayed local date/zone from the saved instant. A changed location timezone or manager profile must not silently reinterpret a paid booking. `visit_event` records actor, acting-for role, actual event time, server receipt time, method, checklist answers, upload/evidence IDs, and expected revision. Do not overwrite a single enum to mean both physical attendance and review outcome. Condition evidence may be captured before/after; specific required tasks come only from the saved per-visit duty snapshot. Server and client share the same required-item/photo validation and human-readable 422 field errors.

**Proposed minimum schema/API shape:** add `scheduledStartAt`, `scheduledEndAt`, `timezone`, `startOffsetMinutes`, `endOffsetMinutes`, `scheduleRevision`, `arrivalReportedAt`, `departureReportedAt`, `inspectionStatus`, `inspectionDueAt`, and `inspectionClosedAt` to `kitchenBookingVisits`; add `serviceEndedAt`, `closeoutStatus`, `closeoutAt`, `settlementModel`, and saved release deadline to `kitchenBookings` (or a one-to-one closeout row if payment ownership requires it). An append-only `kitchen_visit_events`/evidence table holds actor and upload metadata; incident and claim tables retain their own IDs and state. Index due inspection and service-end work by due instant and state; use a uniqueness constraint on `(bookingId, blockIndex)` and event idempotency key. Do not copy raw photo URLs into multiple mutable status columns. Fields and names are proposals for migration review, not a claim that they exist now (`shared/schema.ts:541,583-653,2074`).

| Scheduled service | upcoming → available (arrival window) → in booked time at scheduled start → use right expires at scheduled end → booking **Ended at last visit end + grace** (or approved changed end + grace) | This is derived from saved instants and current time; no check-in/out tap changes it. Grace changes presentation/escalation, not inventory ownership or booked price. |
| Attendance/evidence | unknown; self/assisted arrival reported; self/assisted departure reported; manager/customer later attendance statement; disputed/corrected | No tap leaves attendance **unknown**, never automatically “no-show.” Record who asserted what, when, and with what proof. Both parties can see a correction. |
| Inspection | not due → due at reported departure or scheduled end → cleared / auto-closed uninspected / timely issue filed; issue may enter a separate claim | “Auto-closed uninspected” must never read “manager approved” or “no damage.” An early departure cannot erase the scheduled use right or customer remedy period. |
| Incident and claim | access/safety/equipment/cleanliness/overstay report → triage → remedy or disputed case; claim draft → submitted → response/review/payment resolution | Report is not a charge. Draft expiry, response period, maximum case SLA, and release hold are saved, bounded, and scoped to this booking. |
| Settlement | captured → eligible after saved service/inspection/remedy gates → transfer pending/verified; bank payout tracked separately | Completion, claim liability, transfer to Express, and bank receipt are distinct. An early clear cannot close the customer remedy gate. |

**Clock algorithm:** on every read and command, compute the effective phase from saved instants and revision; a bounded due-work sweep persists transitions/notifications for audit and finance. A delayed worker never moves a deadline. At last scheduled end, the use right expires; at last end plus grace, persist booking Ended idempotently. At inspection deadline, auto-close only ordinary unresolved inspection; if timely access/remedy/claim case exists, close service but hold only the affected financial allocation with a named Local Cooks owner and maximum deadline. Pending claim drafts expire without extending the clock. Settlement eligibility is evaluated from authoritative payment/incident/refund/transfer state, not a `completed` label alone. A server job and on-demand command share an idempotent transition service, so a missed worker tick cannot leave the UI “Confirmed” forever.

### Edge-case contract

| Situation | Chef-facing action and outcome | Manager / Local Cooks outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Late arrival or no arrival tap | “Your booked time ends at 6:00 p.m.”; optional “I arrived” remains available during the visit, then assisted/history report. No automatic guilt or refund. | Manager sees “arrival unconfirmed,” may contact/report evidence after scheduled end; never charge or mark no-show from silence. |
| Forgot departure / early leave | “I left at…” can be recorded within the evidence window; early departure stops reminders, not the booked schedule or automatic refund. | Inspection becomes due at reported departure or scheduled end; auto-close has a fixed maximum. |
| Overstay, next slot free | Offer a priced extension only if inventory, kitchen approval, saved terms, and payment/consent succeed. Otherwise request departure and open a reasoned incident. | Compare actual evidence to schedule; quote and review any disclosed overstay charge separately, with dispute path. |
| Overstay, next booking imminent | No auto-extension; urgent manager and Local Cooks task protects the next person, provides alternative/rebooking or remedy, and records incident. | Never pretend the second booking is available because the first clock ended. Operational occupancy and financial service end are different facts. |
| Kitchen locked, dirty, unsafe, or not ready; manager absent | Primary “Can't get in / kitchen not ready” action at the door; timestamp, optional photo, call/message, retry, “I still can't enter.” Do not ask the person to self-cancel under their penalty policy. | Immediate manager task and Local Cooks escalation after a short saved response deadline; affected payment held for supplier-failure remedy. Safety emergency text directs local emergency services first, then Local Cooks. |
| Equipment broken or supplies missing | Report affected item and whether safe kitchen work can continue; optional photo. | Disable unsafe equipment for future slots; quote item/partial remedy without ending unrelated kitchen time. |
| Damage or unusual cleaning found later | Show claim, evidence, exact proposed amount, response deadline, and dispute action. | Timely new claim remains possible after ordinary closeout under approved rules; later claim is separate payment/recovery, never an unbounded hold. Compare start/end evidence and normal wear. |
| Attendance, damage, or charge disputed | Upload counterevidence and message; no automatic card charge from a disputed report. | Local Cooks case owns decision, saved maximum SLA, audit trail, and verified payment status. |
| Multiple visits and overnight | Today card selects the visit whose local operating date/time is active; show “Visit 2 of 3,” each local date and zone, and next visit. | Each visit gets condition/inspection; booking service end is the latest saved instant. One missed tap cannot block later visit or whole-booking closeout. |
| DST / two kitchens or zones | Display date, time, zone abbreviation and IANA zone where ambiguity matters; countdown uses UTC instants. | Reject nonexistent wall times; save chosen offset for repeated hour. A booking across kitchens must be modeled as distinct location-bound visits/bookings, not inherit one zone. |
| Offline / upload failure | Previously delivered access instructions are cached securely for the booked window. Local draft records text, selected items, photo metadata and capture time; show “Saved on this device—send when connected.” A required upload failure offers retry and manager/Local Cooks assistance without hiding entry help. | Do not mark server-confirmed until ACK. Server receives idempotency key and client capture time, records actual receipt, and flags late evidence for review. Protect sensitive codes from stale cache after cancellation. |
| Cancelled booking page still open | Stale action POST returns 409 with current state; screen refreshes and removes entry codes/actions. | Preserve already recorded evidence; no new access. |
| D11 assisted action | Show “Recorded by [name] from Local Cooks for [person], at [reported time]” and correction link. | Named staff identity, `acting_for`, requester, reason, evidence, expected revision, permission and recipient notices. No impersonated user token or direct status edit. |

## 5. Screen-by-screen experience

### Person booking the kitchen (mobile first)

1. **Confirmed booking / prep:** date, local zone, full address, parking/loading, access method, contact, kitchen rules, booked equipment and reset obligations. A compact “Before you arrive” section shows only configured tasks. Sensitive code appears only within the authorized release window and is removed on cancellation. Manager publishes a backup access contact/method before listing can go live. Example: “Priya, your kitchen is ready for Monday, 5:00–6:00 p.m. NDT. Entry details unlock before your visit.”
2. **Today card (top of bookings and detail):** one large contextual action at a time, plus persistent **Get help**. Before start: “Starts in 20 min” (clock based on saved UTC) and “View entry instructions.” In arrival window: “I'm here” **only if configured**, with secondary “Report an entry problem.” During use: “Booked until 6:00 p.m. NDT” and “Report a problem”; optional departure task becomes primary near the end. After end: “Your booked time ended at 6:00 p.m.” and, if evidence is still open, “Add departure details.” For no configured tasks, no “required” banner, no empty checklist, no mandatory photo label. Visit 2 of 3 is explicit.
3. **Arrival sheet:** first screen shows door/lockbox steps, manager contact, and offline-safe fallback. The optional condition step is short: required saved items first, photos only for configured requirements, plus “Add a photo” as optional evidence. Submit button says “Record arrival”; field-level validation explains every block, with focus moved to the first error. On success, close sheet, update Today/header/detail/list from one read model, announce “Arrival recorded at 5:03 p.m.” Failure shows the server error and retains the draft. No legal analysis in the action sheet; link to “How visit records work.”
4. **In session / issue:** “Report a problem” offers access, cleanliness, equipment, safety, and other; safety appears first with local emergency guidance. Show manager response timer and Local Cooks escalation clearly. Messaging and photos attach to one incident. A report never turns itself into a cancellation or charge.
5. **Departure:** prominent reset guidance **before** the button, from snapshotted kitchen rules; checklist/photo requirements only if configured. “I have left” records actual departure and sends the manager an inspection task. Early leave copy says booked time/price remain per accepted terms. If upload fails, keep draft and offer assistance. Afterwards show “Inspection due by…” and distinguish “Manager cleared,” “Closed after response window without inspection,” and “Issue under review.” A review prompt appears after service end, independent of the evidence tap.

### Kitchen manager

1. **Today / Now board:** make this the first operational block in manager Overview and a persistent `Today` navigation destination. Filter by location, kitchen, and local date. Four groups: **Arriving**, **In session**, **Inspect next**, **Overdue / needs help**. Each row shows the person's name, kitchen, visit number, local time/zone, access issue flag, actual arrival/departure evidence, and next actor. “Arrival unconfirmed” is neutral. Empty state says “No visits at this location today.” Use one aggregate query, not per-booking expansion.
2. **Row detail / inspection:** show schedule and status, saved access/cleanliness rules, before/after photos side by side, answers, messages and event timestamps. Primary action “Clear this visit” when inspection is due; secondary “Report an issue.” One-tap clear uses a short confirmation sheet that identifies the visit and says it records **no issue observed**, not that money is instantly paid. A manager can record assisted arrival/departure with actual time and reason from the same row. If no checkout was submitted, manager can still inspect/report after scheduled end; the scheduled closeout clock proceeds.
3. **Exception tasks:** locked-out alert is above ordinary inspection, with contact/resolve/escalate actions. Overstay with next booking is urgent; capacity and next person's remedy are visible. Claim creation is a separate guided path with evidence, amount, disclosure, response window, and explicit “Draft” versus “Submitted.” Manager may correct a report with a reason; the old event remains.
4. **Setup:** merge the kitchen's arrival/departure settings and location access guide into one guided “Guest arrival and handoff” area. Explain default optionality, preview the person's day-of card, show counts (“0 required photos”), require a tested backup entry method, and snapshot changes for future bookings. Put long rules and optional evidence choices in setup and detail, not in the primary door flow.

### Local Cooks support

One searchable booking case combines commitment, each visit's scheduled instants and zone, attendance assertions, condition media, messages, incident/claim/refund, transfer and bank payout **as separate axes**. Show source and last verified time for each. Support may open an access incident, coordinate an alternative kitchen, record an assisted event, correct a false no-show, close an inspection, issue a scoped remedy, or hold/release a settlement through guarded services per D11. Every action captures named actor, acting-for party, requester, reason, evidence, expected version, provider operation ID when relevant, and notices to both parties. Read-only view-as has a permanent banner and no mutations. Queues prioritize locked-out now, next-booking conflict, unresolved safety, remedy deadline, overdue inspection/case, then transfer exception. The historical-review queue should be under booking support, not tours.

## 6. Notifications and Hobby-cost design

All offsets below are proposed and must be saved with visit schedule revision, local zone and recipient. Use durable SQL outbox/due-work with an event wakeup for immediacy, a low-frequency sweep as backstop, current-state/permission re-read at send time, recipient-specific idempotency, bounded retry, and operator recovery for undeliverable critical mail. Do not send SMS. Every email includes the current person's name, kitchen, local date/time/zone, one useful action, and truthful payment wording. Email does not substitute for the in-app task; “sent” is not “read.” Suppress stale notices after cancel, reschedule, role change or issue resolution (`booking-target-workflows.md`, communication matrix).

| Event | In-app / email timing and audience |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmation | Immediate to both: booked schedule, money charged, access release timing, rules, contact; manager readiness task. |
| Pre-arrival | Proposed 24 hours before start, or immediately for short-notice bookings: person gets route/parking, access and help; manager gets readiness and backup-entry check. Optional day-of reminder 1 hour before only if useful. |
| Arrival window / start | In-app Today card updates from clock; email only if configured required arrival evidence remains missing after start plus 10 minutes, and only once. Manager sees neutral unconfirmed status; never email an automatic no-show allegation. |
| Access/safety/equipment incident | Immediate in-app and email to manager and Local Cooks; acknowledge to reporting person. Escalate at saved response deadline, suppress when resolved. Safety copy prioritizes emergency services. |
| Departure reminder | In-app 15 minutes before end; one email at end only for configured departure obligations if still relevant. No repeated five-minute nudges. |
| Departure reported / scheduled end | Immediate inspection task to manager, acknowledgement to person. At scheduled end with no departure, show inspection due based on time; no no-show claim. |
| Inspection clear / auto-close / issue | Immediate factual state to both; auto-close explicitly says “response window ended without an inspection.” A claim gets its own evidence and response email. |
| Remedy / claim / overstay / settlement | Separate event identities and deadlines. Exact amount/decision/payment status to both; manager sees “Transfer to Express initiated” only after verified transfer, never “in bank” until verified payout. |
| Review | After the last visit's scheduled end, when the experience is eligible; avoid sending to a party whose booking was cancelled before use or whose safety case makes a generic prompt insensitive. |

**Runtime:** remove the 5/10/15/30-second overlapping polls from visit, manager table and overview. A consolidated `GET /api/.../visits/today` and booking detail read model should return `nextChangeAt` and an entity version. Refresh on mutation success, page focus/reconnect, location/filter change and a timer at the next local UI transition; a visible board may use a **60–120-second** conditional/ETag poll as a backstop, stopped in hidden tabs. SSE can deliver low-latency event invalidation if Vercel Hobby connection duration and concurrency are measured; it is optional, not the source of truth, and should replace rather than supplement tight polling. The Inngest/SQL worker should process only due rows in bounded batches and avoid one invocation per visit reminder. Existing 30-minute staging / 15-minute production backstop policy in `PHASE0_LOG.md` is a recovery mechanism, not a promise of minute-perfect notices.
