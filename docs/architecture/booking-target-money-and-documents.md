# Proposed payments, settlement and financial documents

2026-10-11. Supporting specification for [the consolidated proposal](booking-target-architecture.md). Target design is J, not implemented or approved. F/R/E/Q labels follow the main document. Current object reconstruction and source symbols: [Stripe current state](booking-stripe-current-state.md). No Stripe resource API was called during this proposal; test keys alone do not verify connected accounts or webhook routing.

## Proven shape and current external limits

**R:** `stripe-connect-service.ts` creates Express accounts in Canada. Checkout manually captures a platform PaymentIntent, with no destination `transfer_data`, `application_fee_amount` or `on_behalf_of` in the inspected current path. `stripe-transfer-service.ts` later creates a separate Transfer using the source Charge, stable payment-intent key and actual balance-transaction fee. `routes/webhooks.ts` invokes it shortly after capture/fee availability. Express is the account experience; separate charges and transfers is the charge model. Existing account countries/settings remain Q.

**E:** In separate charges/transfers, platform charges and transfers are decoupled; the platform balance bears processor fees, refunds and chargebacks. `transfer_group` is association, not a money lock; `source_transaction` links fund availability. Failed transfers need an explicit retry. Omitting `on_behalf_of` makes the platform business of record **for the payment**; this does not settle the rental supplier/tax contract. [Stripe separate charges/transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers?locale=es-ES).

**E:** Stripe documents holding funds before transfer or before connected payout for a defined purpose such as service completion, discourages arbitrary withholding, and gives a 90-day reserve-holding limit outside the US/Thailand. Check actual Accounts v1 `controller.losses.payments` or v2 responsibility property for connected negative-balance ownership. Account-specific holding clock/application needs provider confirmation before launch. [Stripe account balances](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/account-balances).

**E:** Manual connected payouts stop automatic bank payouts, have country deadlines (Canada falls in the 90-day group), and require the Payout API. They are not escrow; Transfers and Payouts move money between different endpoints. [Stripe manual payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manual-payouts). Automatic payout interval and settlement delay are separate. A delay override up to 31 days applies to direct/`on_behalf_of` settlement timing; it is not a booking-aware gate for this integration. [Payout schedule](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manage-payout-schedule).

**E:** Express users can change schedules or pay themselves if those dashboard features are enabled. Therefore manual schedule alone is not proof that manager withdrawals are blocked. [Express Dashboard](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/express-dashboard).

**E:** Authorization has a card/transaction-specific expiry, exposed as Charge `payment_method_details.card.capture_before`. Do not assume a universal seven-day hold, reserve until a distant booking ends, or use extended-authorization/private-preview features without verified eligibility. Partial capture normally releases the remainder. [Stripe authorization/capture](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method).

## Object and funds map [J, building on R]

```text
Chef card → platform Checkout Session → platform PaymentIntent / Charge
                                      → platform BalanceTransaction (actual fee/net)
                                      → immutable booking component allocations

Before capture: cancel original PI → authorization release (not a refund)
After capture: refund original Charge/PI → customer original payment method

After release gate: platform → Transfer(source_transaction=original Charge)
                            → kitchen Express balance
                            → Stripe Payout → kitchen bank

After transfer remedy: customer refund from platform; separate reversal/recovery
Damage award: separate consented PI/payment → separate allocation/Transfer/document
```

Record Session, PI, Charge, BalanceTransaction, Refund, Transfer, Reversal and Payout IDs with environment/account scope, currency, amount, operation ID and verified status. A request accepted by an API is an observation, not final payment outcome. A Transfer is not a bank Payout. `charge.dispute.*` is a card dispute, separate from an application damage claim.

## Request and capture

Quote transaction validates final entitlement, requirements, kitchen license, operating date/time, price/tax/fee versions, active add-ons and actual quantity/exclusivity. Reserve requested kitchen and constrained resources together under locks. Checkout hold and Checkout expiry must have a documented relationship; current 37/32 minute defaults [R] are not new accepted constants.

Authorized fulfillment commits a pending request, actual authorization identity/amount, accepted snapshot, reservation deadline and outbox atomically. Any fulfillment failure after authorization records compensation first, then cancels the original PI or refunds an unexpectedly captured amount and verifies the provider result. Do not repair by making another charge.

Set response deadline to the minimum of product response SLA, session-start-minus-approved-preparation-margin, and actual-capture-before-minus-approved-provider-margin. Missing/invalid provider deadline blocks ordinary acceptance pending reconciliation; user sees review state. A last-minute request needs enough time for a real decision; otherwise explain unavailability or allow a disclosed manager-coordinated request, never a silent late capture exception.

Manager acceptance freezes exactly accepted allocations and persists a capture operation. Partial capture is allowed only for items the customer explicitly permitted to be declined; re-derive fee/tax for accepted components using the original basis and rounding, never charge tax/fee on declined value or above recomputed consented amount. Verify authorized amount, currency, identity, deadlines and inventory immediately before capture; compensation handles deadline/race conflicts. On succeeded exact capture, commit confirmation, receipt and outbox. A lost reply goes to lookup/reconciliation. Product guard refuses initiating capture at/after its deadline; distributed provider completion may arrive late despite margins, so late success is an explicit incident/recovery state, not a claim of impossible network timing guarantees. A free booking follows the same access/inventory/terms controls with `payment_not_required`; never fabricate a paid receipt or Stripe object.

Authentication can succeed on request yet an additional off-session payment later require action. Keep an explicit customer recovery link and expiry; do not mark modification or damage collection paid from API submission. Store consent evidence for future use separately from the mere presence of a saved payment method.

## Booking money allocation and platform fee

For each charge, preserve kitchen rental, accepted equipment/storage components, each applicable tax component, platform fee and any lawful tax on that fee. Fee owner/basis and refundable eligibility must be explicit. Admin fee changes affect new quotes only; location tax changes likewise do not recalculate accepted payments. Manager-controlled tax setting [F] is not validation that any arbitrary number is legally correct. Validate registration/status and allowed configuration with an accountant-approved matrix; don't assume a zero rate equals a registered zero-rated supply.

**Fee display rule [F/J]:** use the charged fee on that financial revision. Explicit zero means no platform-fee row, percentage badge, refund checkbox, zero-fee email line or document line across customer/manager/admin transaction surfaces. Configuration controls can still show the admin's zero setting. Positive historical fee remains visible/refundable even when current admin setting becomes zero. A fee refunded to zero still has a meaningful original charge/refund history; don't erase it. Original zero differs from positive charged then refunded.

Actual processor fees are business cost, distinctly labelled in manager net statements. Estimated 2.9%+30 cents or a helper's `totalPlatformFee` is not actual fee truth and must not create a commission at zero. Derive transfer from verified recorded allocations, actual fee and already-refunded/previously-settled amounts. Never estimate a final bank statement from mutable rates. For a new-policy cohort, positive-fee publication and tax collection require the validated supplier/registration/basis matrix and manager agreement for processor burden, including tax/fee portions. Until validated, do not enable those new configurations or accept a paid taxable supply by pretending zero tax resolves unknown obligations; affected locations remain draft/payment-disabled pending review. Existing transactions need lawful correction/recovery, not silent blanket reconfiguration.

Example [illustration, not a provider rate]: customer pays rental 100, tax 15, fee 0; actual Stripe fee 3.64. If agreed ordinary manager cost allocation remains current, manager gross 115 minus processor 3.64 = 111.36 net. An unused supply failure owes customer 115; 111.36 retained net plus 3.64 funded platform cost covers it. When fee is positive, its return is foregone platform revenue, not another Stripe fee. Fee tax/manager processor burden on fee portion needs explicit approved agreement; do not silently shift it.

## Refund, reversal and loss responsibility

**E:** Uncaptured authorization is canceled rather than refunded. Captured refund debits the platform for this charge model; transfers require separate reversal for recovery. Refunds return to the original method. Refunds can remain pending/fail, and original processing fees generally are not returned; early reversals are an exception, not a budget guarantee. [Stripe refunds](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds).

| Event [J] | Customer remedy | Economic owner/default | Operational payer/recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Withdraw/decline/timeout before capture | Release hold; no captured-money refund | No normal captured processor expense | Cancel original PI, verify released; issuer timing caveat |
| Chef timely P-A cancellation | Full affected gross including positive fee/tax | Kitchen loses undelivered revenue; platform returns fee and absorbs sunk fee under startup budget | Platform refund before transfer; no reversal needed |
| Chef late cancellation/no-show | Saved retained/refundable line-item schedule | Customer bears agreed retention; no invented penalty | Refund eligible balance and settle retained kitchen share |
| Kitchen unable to honour unused booking | Full affected gross; optional alternative with customer consent | Kitchen undelivered revenue removed; platform fee returned; startup processor loss platform unless approved contract recovery | Refund cannot wait for manager collection |
| Platform double allocation/error | Full affected remedy | Platform costs, including any approved manager compensation | Platform executes; incident tracked separately |
| Partial use / unused essential item / safety dispute | Evidence-based scoped remedy under disclosed protection rules | Admin reasoned allocation; don't blame button actor | Hold relevant settlement, case resolution and adjustment |
| Damage award | No unilateral deduction from unrelated refund; separately consented/authorized collection | Liable party only after decision; collection failure not platform-insured by default | Separate payment attempt and claim receipt, no automatic guaranteed reimbursement |
| Card dispute after transfer | Avoid overlapping duplicate refund; issuer/card process controls outcome | Platform charge exposure; connected liability verified from account properties | Operator preserves evidence, reserves exposure, recovers only contractually/technically possible |

**Pre-transfer card dispute [J/Q]:** separate payment fraud from claimed non-delivery. Recommend Local Cooks bears payment-rail/fraud loss for an evidenced compliant delivered booking and pays the kitchen on the approved schedule from funded platform liquidity; do not silently assign every chargeback to the kitchen by withholding earnings. A kitchen-caused non-delivery dispute follows the service-remedy decision/recovery agreement, with only affected value temporarily held within the case SLA. This principal-loss recommendation needs an explicitly funded owner-approved risk budget/manager contract, not merely the modest processor-fee budget. Card issuer dispute completion may outlast the release SLA; operator prevents double refund, retains claim/chargeback evidence and funds obligations without resetting holding clocks.

Refund operation stores immutable entitlement, component allocation, requested amount, case/source IDs and prior refunds. On committed cancellation release future session inventory, retain occupied physical storage separately, block new conflicting settlement, and reconcile original charge. Provider failure never restores the booking. Admin may correct a mistaken amount using a new reasoned operation; no mutation of an already-executed refund identity.

For legacy already-transferred bookings: platform executes owed customer remedy from funded float while separately reversing/recovering allocated kitchen money. Do not make reversal success the prerequisite for an earned refund. Successful reversal followed by refund failure creates a recorded recovery obligation back to the appropriate beneficiary; it cannot disappear. Stable IDs and cumulative totals prevent reversing or refunding twice.

## Settlement release and treasury

Each booking records settlement-model version and deadline source. A paid cancelled booking with retained kitchen amount uses a cancellation settlement path; it need not wait for a visit that will never occur, but refunds/cases must reconcile first. Zero net produces a verified no-transfer result, not endless retries.

**One transfer guard:** the locked source operation (`stripe-transfer-service.ts:transferLockedSource`) must enforce recorded model and release eligibility, regardless of caller. Seven booking-path caller sites were identified across `routes/webhooks.ts` and `payment-transactions-service.ts`, plus separate claim/overstay reuse [R]. Guard all deployed code before enrolling B; old serverless instances cannot be assumed upgraded. Prove old executors/endpoints are drained or excluded, including in-flight requests, before any B source exists; a new guard cannot constrain an old binary that lacks it. Rollback may stop enrollment but cannot roll money execution back to pre-guard code while B obligations exist. Snapshot model atomically at fulfillment. Missing model on provable pre-cutover source is classified legacy; a post-cutover missing/ambiguous marker becomes a named deadlined review obligation, never silent early transfer or endless hold. Claim/overstay sources declare their own model; not every call is a deferred kitchen-booking settlement. Caller inventory plus stale-webhook/fee-sync/replay tests prove the central guard.

**Case SLA and fallback:** publish normal release and a timely-case exception with its own maximum decision deadline. Escalate before that deadline, not near the provider ceiling. Decide service/refund/earned allocation from evidence. If operations cannot decide by the contractual case maximum, the owner must fund the agreed provisional remedy: customer receives established/provisionally awarded remedy without duplicate dispute payment; kitchen receives its contractually payable allocation on schedule, with disputed recovery recorded separately. This may put full principal, not just processor cost, at platform risk. The contract must define that provisional outcome and appeal/recovery, and the funded budget must cover it before enrollment; absent that capability B is not launchable. Repeated drafts, messages, appeals or late staff action cannot create a new withholding clock.

**Supplier/payee binding:** freeze verified supplier legal identity and connected account ID, account country/currency/ownership evidence and invoice identity on the accepted financial source. Current schema binds Connect ID to `users` [R]; deferred payment stretches account-change risk. Profile edits/reassignment never reroute earned money. Ownership sale, re-onboarding or capability mismatch opens an audited beneficiary-change case with business evidence and lawful approval. Do not pay a departed individual merely because their old user ID appears on the booking; disputed beneficiary identity becomes a cure obligation, not an arbitrary permanent freeze.

For serviced bookings, eligibility requires verified captured allocations, scheduled end passed, inspection cleared/auto-close fallback satisfied, saved customer-remedy window expired, no timely open affected service case, no unverified refund/capture/dispute overlap, destination identity/capability known, actual costs resolved and held-funds limit safe. `payouts_enabled=false` and `transfers` capability are distinct: inspect the reason before deciding whether transfer is allowed or should remain held. Offboarded/reassigned manager does not silently change the financial beneficiary.

Serialize cancellation/refund/settlement under one booking/payment operation guard. Reserve transferable cents and stable operation key before provider IO. Re-check current refund/case/dispute facts before the request and compensate any unavoidable distributed race after it. Provider success followed by SQL failure is reconciled from the original key/object. A universal exactly-once claim across SQL/Stripe is not made.

**B launch gate:** treasury must prove retained customer/manager liabilities cannot be swept into spendable operating cash. Inspect platform payout schedule, available/pending balances, provider reserves and automatic debits; protect funds using a supported arrangement and independent treasury controls. A source Charge association does not replace protected liquidity. Held earnings are a payable, not revenue. Refund float includes sunk processor loss and gross amounts no longer held, disputed exposure and settlement latency.

**Canadian payment-regulation gate [E/I/Q]:** the RPAA defines holding end-user funds as a payment function. Current Bank of Canada marketplace guidance distinguishes marketplace custody in its controlled bank account from end-to-end handling by third-party PSPs. Counsel must classify this actual flow, registration/safeguarding obligations and permitted custody before enrollment; no automatic exemption is inferred from Stripe, nor is Local Cooks declared a regulated PSP solely because a Stripe balance exists. Prefer keeping protected funds within the approved provider flow, avoiding sweeps through a Local Cooks operating bank account. This assessment can affect A/C as well as B. [RPAA](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/r-7.36/FullText.html), [Bank of Canada marketplace policy](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2025/12/supervisory-policy-for-online-marketplaces/).

Holding deadline is computed from verified applicable provider rules, original receipt of funds and conservative margin. Never extend it by moving money between accounts, changing policy, closing/reopening a case or migrating a booking. The 90-day documentation is a ceiling to verify, not permission to hold every Canadian booking for 90 days.

For launch, recommend restricting the complete projected cycle: capture → **last service/visit or attached component end** → closeout/remedy → bounded case/cure resolution → provider margin. Validate at quote and again before capture against the applicable original-funds ceiling; lead time to first session alone is insufficient. Shared-PI storage/multiple visits require explicit allocation/release ownership; don't transfer the full PI at kitchen end while another component is still owed, and don't silently convert the existing single transfer into multiple partial transfers. Per-component settlement, if necessary, needs approved operation identities/cumulative limits; otherwise block unsupported spans at quote. Exact booking horizon/span is an owner decision after provider confirmation. Far-ahead bookings need separately approved staged-payment or early-transfer/funded-recovery terms, not an expiring card authorization. Before a deadline, resolve/refund an incident or follow a provider/legal-approved funded remedy; do not auto-transfer disputed money blindly. Escalations are operator tasks, not new discretionary withholding rights.

**Non-dispute cure obligations:** a rejected/closed/transfer-disabled payee and a failed customer refund each have beneficiary notice, named owner, cure deadline/escalation and a lawful verified alternative-payment/unclaimed-funds runbook. Failed-refund amount remains a customer liability, excluded from manager settlement and operating cash. Bank/card details are verified through a secure path; no silent account substitution. Alternative payout/refund method and unclaimed-funds rules need legal/provider approval before B launch; review status alone is not a resolution. Every retained obligation remains counted against protected liability and original holding clock.

## Receipt, invoice, adjustment and payout statement

**E:** Stripe receipts evidence successful payment/refund; one-time Checkout invoice creation is optional and separately priced. Platform charge-model receipts use platform branding/email settings. Manual-capture compatibility of automatic Checkout invoice generation must be verified; current code comment is not authoritative documentation. [Stripe receipts and paid invoices](https://docs.stripe.com/receipts).

| Document [J] | Meaning | When | Authority/data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote/request acknowledgement | Proposed price or card authorization; no proof of payment | Quote/request | Accepted quote, hold/deadline, no PAID label |
| Payment receipt | Amount successfully captured | After verified capture | Original Charge/captured allocations and issue timestamp |
| Supplier tax invoice, possibly marked paid | Who supplied rental/fee and taxable amount; not a second charge | Accountant-approved statutory/commercial timing, not automatically deferred until closeout | Correct supplier identities/registration, itemized basis/rate/tax, buyer business, original invoice identity |
| Refund/credit adjustment | Changes to original charge/supply and refund progress | Obligation notice immediately; successful-money receipt after provider confirmation; tax adjustment per approved rules | Links original invoice/receipt, component amount/reason, provider state |
| Final booking summary | Delivered/retained/refunded/extra amounts reconciled | Closeout/case completion | Booking revisions and separate money operations; not another invoice creating debt |
| Kitchen settlement statement | Gross, refunded/retained amounts, agreed costs, net and Transfer status | Transfer verified or clearly provisional before release | Allocation ledger/actual fees; bank payout still separate |
| Bank payout statement | Stripe bank movement with included transfers/adjustments | Verified Payout lifecycle | Stripe payout reconciliation; don't attribute entire payout to one booking |
| Damage/extra payment receipt | Separate successful additional charge | After actual success | Claim decision/consent and new original financial source |

Recommend kitchen business as rental supplier and Local Cooks as positive fee supplier, subject to agency/tax agreement approval [J/Q]. Owner confirms marketplace and manager tax configuration, not a legal principal/agent determination. Combined presentation may be convenient, but each supply/issuer must remain clear; Stripe's payment business-of-record label does not settle tax ownership. If Local Cooks is instead the contractual seller, invoices, tax duties and agreements change materially: do not launch with ambiguous issuer. CRA distinguishes agent/vendor tax responsibility and possible joint elections; do not infer a tax election from Connect onboarding. [E: CRA agents and special cases](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-special-cases.html).

CRA requires sufficient written tax evidence for eligible customer input-tax-credit claims. Supplier/registration identity, date, consideration, tax and necessary buyer/payment/supply details matter; a generic payment receipt is not automatically sufficient. The opened detailed memorandum has old monetary thresholds, which are **not adopted**; accountant must validate current thresholds/rules. [CRA documentation requirements](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/8-4/documentary-requirements-claiming-input-tax-credits.html).

Generate immutable document content/version from original financial snapshots and frozen supplier/buyer identity; store issue time rather than download time. Append correction/credit documents instead of overwriting an original. Download can create bytes from frozen content deterministically; no shared R2 write is needed for local acceptance tests. Avoid Stripe Billing unless actual recurring/invoice-payment needs justify it; don't charge the already-paid booking twice. Choose one receipt email strategy after verifying Stripe email settings to avoid redundant or inconsistent paid notices.
