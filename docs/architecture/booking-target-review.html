Three startup options

A · Transfer near capture Smallest settlement change

Fast manager cash. Platform needs funded refunds and recovery after money leaves its control.

B · Transfer after closeout Recommended, conditional

Most early remedies avoid reversals. Requires protected treasury, release deadlines and approved manager terms.

C · Connected manual payouts Conditional fallback

Account-wide controls add allocation, payout and dashboard-permission complexity. Manual mode alone is insufficient.

Release gate before launch: verify supplier/payee ownership, central transfer guard, actual Stripe controls, custody/legal assessment, release deadlines and funded refund/dispute liquidity.

Full affected refund means the customer gets the full affected payment.

Returning a customer’s money and recovering costs from a responsible business are separate operations. A blameless customer must not wait for that recovery.

Damages are a separate case

Evidence → customer response → reasoned decision → consented collection. Silence does not approve a charge.

Receipt now; final summary later.

Proof of payment follows verified capture. Supplier tax invoice timing needs accounting confirmation. Closeout produces the final reconciliation and manager settlement statement.

Keep three clocks distinct

Manager inspection, customer service-remedy report and later damage filing. Holding kitchen earnings cannot secure a chef’s separate damage debt.

RECOMMENDED FOR APPROVAL

Valid applicable credentials; reasoned suspension and review of existing paid sessions.

Cancellation effective on submission

Saved policy controls entitlement; manager or workflow delay cannot erase it.

Gross-refund standard window

One simple launch schedule; a clearly disclosed net or graded option remains available.

Structured published kitchen rules

Guided use, safety, access and cleaning sections. Supplemental contract optional, disclosed and versioned.

Interim edit lock → future-only versions

Remove the lock only after proven immutable coverage and owner approval.
